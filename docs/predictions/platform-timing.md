# Prediction, written before the test: return timing by platform (F/018–019)

Written and committed on 27 November 2026 before `scripts/bu_timing.py` is
written or run. Nothing in it is changed after the run; the result is
recorded separately in `docs/f-bu-audit.md`.

**Source of the prediction (independent of F/018–019's arrows).** F/013's
column heads group its twelve platforms: "Long time" over N.o 1–2 (t31–t32)
and N.o 11–12 (t41–t42), "Short time" over N.o 3–10 (t33–t40)
(`sources/notes/F013.md` §2). On F/013 itself the closing arrows of the
long-time platform columns fall in row 15 and those of the short-time ones in
row 12 (bands 1 and 3).

**Hypothesis P.** "B k" is a part bolted to travelling platform k, and the part
moves with the platform.

**Prediction.** On F/018–019, a part labelled "B 1" or "B 2" (a long-time
platform) returns (its first double-headed arrow after the B, in its column)
in a later row of its band than a part labelled "B 3"–"B 6" (short-time) whose
B stands in the same cycle. Stated as: every long-time return is in a later
row than every short-time return of the same cycle.

**What would count against it.** Any cycle in which a long-time part returns
in the same row as, or an earlier row than, a short-time part. Cycles with no
long-time part or no short-time part are untestable and are reported as such.

**Caveat known before the test.** The numbering of platforms on F/018–019 is
assumed to be F/013's: figures 3–6 carry F/013's lifts there, and 1–2 carry
the lifts of F/013's platforms 1–2 (seen before this prediction was written).
Whether F/018–019's platforms are grouped in time as F/013's are is the
question; F/018–019 has no platform heads.
