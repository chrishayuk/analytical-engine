**Rules, fixed before the test**
1. **Motion.** The part in a column labelled "B k" in a band's "2/3" row moves
   in row 1 of that band, up if platform k's head lift is positive and down if
   negative, by the figure of that lift. The lifts are F/015's own heads (the
   same as F/013's): 1:+1.1 2:+.6 3:+1.1 4:+.6 5:+.4 6:+.2 7:−.2 8:−.4 9:−.6
   10:−1.1 11:−.6 12:−1.1.
2. **Return.** The part returns, with a double-headed arrow, in row "11–1/3"
   of the same band, as every labelled part on F/013 does (t01, t09, t16,
   t22).
3. **No motion for a lone U.** A column whose only label in a band is "U k"
   has no arrow in row 1 of that band.

Where a label has a struck figure, the figure written after it is used, and
the row is flagged; a miss there is reported as a miss.
