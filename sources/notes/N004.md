## Draft and fair copy compared

The fair copy (223/689) covers 1837 only; the draft (223/669) covers 1837
and 1838. The column headings differ: the draft has Signs, Digit Counting,
Operation Counting, Index Counting, Registering, Selecting Ap., Gen. Plan,
Rack, Zero App., Barrel Drum Cards and an unheaded column; the fair copy
has Addition (Sign, Zero App.), Counting Ap. (Store, Dec^l, Combin^ts),
Selectors & Registering Ap., General Plans (Circular, Rack), Barrels Drums
& Cards, Miscellaneous Contrivances, Notations, Division, Principles. Draft
entries under Digit Counting appear in the fair copy under Counting Ap.;
draft Registering and Selecting entries under Selectors & Registering.

Items in both, with their differences:

- Plan for Engraving Double Engine, 43: same (draft Jan; fair copy Jan).
- "By Stepping on Carriage": draft "p 10 III"; fair copy "p 10 of III".
- "By two fig wh^s …": the draft adds "25 Feb"; the fair copy has no
  day. Draft "19 V III", fair copy "p 19 of V III".
- "By Picking up sectors p 134 III": fair copy adds "of".
- Long pinions / adding wheels, 4 in a cage, 48: wording reordered ("Long
  pinions & adding wh^s / 4 wh^s in cage" against "Long pinions and
  add^ory whs. / 4 Wheels in a cage").
- "Picking up Sectors 44": fair copy "By picking up Sectors", 44.
- "Register p 44 V III / 3 July": fair copy "Register p 44 Vol III 3"
  (the "July" not written).
- "30 Aug Carr^e given up p 79 V III / 17 Aug Moveable wire in lever p 81
  V III": in the fair copy "17 Aug" is placed under the second line.
- "p 73 V III / 25 Aug": fair copy "page 73 V III 25".
- "No 2 Selecting App. p 54 V III 26 50": same.
- "adding wh^s long pinions 31 51 / 2 in each cage": same.
- "No 17 Vert. chain by wire 30 52": same.
- "No 18 Hoarding for Mult^n 4 57": same. "No 19 Do": the draft gives
  "29 Oct 4 57"; the fair copy gives no day or numbers (a faint pencil "2"
  only).
- Grouping for 2 in each cage 4 53; Grouping for plan 15 17 55: same.
- "No 14 Single engine 7 [?56]": fair copy "No 14 Single Engine 7", no
  bold numeral.
- Stepping on Table axes 7 58: same.
- Grouping for plan 16 (57, 58, 59): same.
- "No 15 was not drawn …" and "No 16 Single Engine 23 60": same.

In the fair copy for 1837 but not found on the draft: "Remarks on Count.
