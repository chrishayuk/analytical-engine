# BAB/F/002 — "Multiplication before the invention of multiplication by table", sheet 2 of 2

**No person has checked this transcription.** It was made by a model from
the images listed below. Read every mark against the images before relying on
it. It covers the cover, the whole table (three complete bands of 13 rows and
one partial row after them) and the notes written under the table.

The conventions are those of [`F001.md`](F001.md):

- **Reading**: what is on the paper.
- **Source**: what a cited publication says, with page.
- **Inference**: this note's own guess, labelled. None is used to make the
  sheet "work".

Uncertain readings are marked `[?x]`, illegible text `[illeg.]`. The symbol
numbers (S1 …) are those of `F001.md` section 3 and
[`../../docs/f-concordance.md`](../../docs/f-concordance.md).

## 2. The table has no column heads

The table starts at the top edge of the sheet with row 1 of a band. There is
no heading row, no title, and no "Multiply a, b, c, d" line. The rows are
numbered 1–13 in the left column, three times.

**The column identities therefore come from the ruling, not from heads.**
The ruling matches F/001 column for column:

- dashed rules between d01/d02, d03/d04, d37/d38, d39/d40 and d44/d45, as
  between F/001's c01/c02, c03/c04, c37/c38, c39/c40 and c44/c45;
- heavy rules left of d07, d15 and d36 (F/001 has heavy rules left of c15
  and c36; whether F/001 also has one left of c07 was not checked);
- double rules left of d13, d14, d18, d26, d33 and d53, and the double rule
  left of d53 as F/001 has right of c52;
- the same column widths, at an offset of about −20 to −50 px from 275 on
  304, and about −190 px from 276 on 305.

Marks of the same kind fall in the same columns as on F/001: the four digits
and four complements in d46–d49 and d53–d56; "Carry" in d05; "Gears with
n–k" under the four lower multiplying pinions d18/d20/d22/d24; ".5" beside
d52. In this note F/002's columns are called **d01–d56**, and **dNN is taken
to be the same part as F/001's cNN** for every NN.

**Inference (not on the sheet):** dNN = cNN. The evidence is the ruling and
the way the marks fall. Nothing written on F/002 names a column. The bracketed
names in `mechanical/F002-cycle*.txt` (`d05 [=c05 F Cir.Mo.]`) are F/001's
heads, given for reading only.

## 3. The notes under the table (reading)

Under the last band, two notes are written, each bracketed to a range of
columns by a short stroke ending in a hook at a heavy rule that runs down from
the table.

On 304, bracketed from the heavy rule left of d07 to the heavy rule left of
d15 (so covering **d07–d14**):

> These columns belong to the method of multiplying
> by means of Snails and feelers which distinguish
> each figure, or which have ten angular positions.

On 305, bracketed from the heavy rule left of d36 to the double rule left of
d53 (so covering **d36–d52**):

> These columns belong to the method of multiplying without snails, and with a feeler which has
> only two angular positions.

On 305, right of the double rule, under d53–d56:

> N.B. The comp.<sup>ts</sup> are proposed
> to be used for the purpose of
> avoiding the necessity of making
> U[?1 / ↑] move in two directions.

The letter after "making" is the same outlined capital U as in d51's head on
F/001 ("U … Starting tooth of wheel which drives Registering tooth"),
followed by a mark read as "1" or a small upward arrow.

These are Babbage's own statements about the columns. They are the only
writing on either sheet that says what a group of columns is for.

(`% after-1 | d05 …`), because the parser has no row prefix for a partial row
after the last band.

## 5. What differs between the bands (reading)

- **Digits** in row 2: 0 0 1 1 / 0 0 0 1 / 0 0 0 0; complements 0 0 9 9 /
  0 0 0 9 / 0 0 0 0. In each band one multiplier wheel and one complement wheel
  have a short down arrow beside the digit, and in each case it is the wheel
  whose digit differs from the band before (d48 2→1, d48 1→0, d49 1→0;
  d55 8→9, d55 9→0, d56 9→0).
- **Feeder lowered**: none in band 4; d08 (units) "Lowered upon 1." in band 5;
  none in band 6.
- **"Gears with"**: none in band 4; n–3 … n–6 in band 5; "Put out of gear." in
  band 6.
- **"Rest" words**: band 4 has nine ("Rest.", "Rest out.", "Rest in.") where
  bands 5 and 6 have arrows with "Put in."/"Put out."/"active.".
- **Dashed shafts**: band 4 only (d36 row 4, d38 rows 9–10).
- **IF and the converging double arrow**: band 6 only.
