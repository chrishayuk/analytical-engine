## The cover (222/586)

Top right: "No 18", "Copied from Not. No 17 Dated 1 Oct. 1838". Under the
title: "9 Aug. 1841". Bottom right: "E.-1905", "BAB [L] 18".

"Notation For Computing the Elliptic radius vector by having the
eccentricity ε & mean Anomaly Ξ". "Formula":

- ρ = A0 + A1 cos Ξ + A2 cos 2Ξ + A3 cos 3Ξ + &c + An cos nΞ + &c
- An = Bn,n εⁿ + Bn,n+2 εⁿ⁺² + &c + Bn,n+2p εⁿ⁺²ᵖ + &c
- A0 = a(1 + ε²/2)
- Bn,n = −nⁿ⁻²/(1·2·3…(n−1)·2ⁿ⁻¹) a;
  Bn+1,n+1 = −(n+1)ⁿ⁻¹/(1·2·3…n·2ⁿ) a = ½((n+1)/n)ⁿ⁻¹ Bn,n
- Bn,n+2p = (−1)ᵖ⁺¹ (n+2p)(n+2p−1)…(n+p+1) nⁿ⁻²⁺²ᵖ /
  (1·2·3…(n−1+2p)·1·2·3…p·2ⁿ⁻¹⁺²ᵖ) a
- Bn,n+2p+2 = … = −n²(n+2+2p)/(4(1+p)(1+n+p)(n+2p)) Bn,n+2p = −(Pp/Qp) Bn,n+2p
- Pp = n²(n+2+2p) = 2n²p + &c; ΔP = 2n²; Δ²P = 0
- Qp = 4(1+p)(1+n+p)(n+2p), expanded over four lines to
  8p³ + (16+12n)p² + (8+16n+4n²)p + &c; ΔQp = 24p² + 8(7+3n)p + 4(8+7n+n²);
  Δ²Qp = 48p + 8(10+3n); Δ³Qp = 48; Δ⁴Qp = 0
- "Let n = 6, p = 0": P0 = 288, Q0 = 168, ΔP = 72, ΔQ0 = 344, Δ²Q0 = 224,
  Δ³Q0 = 48
- B6,6 = −6⁴/(1·2·3·4·5·2⁵) a = −2⁴3⁴a/(2⁸·3·5) = −3³a/(2⁴5) = −27a/80

Every figure of the "Let" line agrees with the formulas at n = 6, p = 0.

## Run

With the Bs entered without their signs, as the sheet holds them, and each
B, P, Q and difference defined from the cover's formulas at n = 6 (not from
the table's recurrence): 91 statements, 58 agree. Of the 33 that do not:

- 27 are cells where a number read in the row is written again, unchanged,
  in another column (above). The engine allows a number read to be laid in
  another column only as its complement. These are a limit of the engine,
  not disagreements of the sheet: every one of them is the number read.
- 6 are the sheet's: row 5 (the mill and v14: a × on P1 and Q1 cannot give
  P1/Q1), row 6 (the mill, v13 and v17: a ÷ cannot give (P1/Q1)×B6,8), and
  row 20 (the mill "ΔQ2 + ΔQ2").

Every B the table makes, B6,8 to B6,18, agrees with the cover's general
formula; every P, Q, ΔQ, Δ²Q with the cover's formulas. The run counts 12
great and 17 small operations, and six tables of multiples, none used twice.

## How it differs from BAB/L/017

The cover says No 18 is "Copied from Not. No 17 Dated 1 Oct. 1838", and is
itself dated 9 Aug. 1841: a fair copy, nearly three years later, of No 17.
How the copy differs from No 17 can be seen only beside the transcription of
L/017 (made separately).
