## The cover (222/658)

"No 8 Aug 20th /37". "Calculation of Tn = n² + 2n + 3 By Differences",
"T0 = 3 Δ0 = 3 Δ³n = 0 / Δ²0 = 3", and "It requires four operations to obtain
each value of Tn or its complement according as n is even or odd".

The cover's Δ²0 = 3 is read as a 3 at full size. The table's heading has
Δ²T0 = 2, which is right for n² + 2n + 3; the cover's figure is a slip.

## The table (222/659)

Heading: "Calculation of a rational Integral Function, of the Second Degree
by Differences"; "Tn = an² + bn + c, Let a = 1, b = 2, c = 3, then Tn = an² +
bn + c = 1n² + 2n + 3, T0 = 3, ΔT0 = 3, Δ²T0 = 2".

Not a table of the mill. Columns: the number of the turn (1 to 16); a narrow
column with × between turns 4/5, 8/9, 12/13 and after 16; "Carriages
Negative", three columns "Car"; the store v1 "Δ²", v2 "Δ", v3 "T", v4, v5,
an unheaded column, v6 "Δ²", v7 "Δ", v8 "T", v9-v12. Numbers are written on
three figures (002, 998). A complement carries a small figure, the number it
is the complement of: 998₂, 995₅, 994₆, 991₉, 982₁₈. "−0" is a carriage at
negative zero. The table is in `corpus/L008.bab`, as comments.

- Turns 9 and 13: "−0" in the third carriage, faint, in pencil; the
  instructions do not mention it, but it is what makes turns 11 and 15 a
  transfer to a carriage at zero.
- The right-hand column marks with * the turns that give a value of T
  (4: Ar Co T1, 8: T2, 16: T4; 12 gives Ar Co T3 without the star).
- The right-hand column counts the numbers on each carriage with accents
  (1st Car', Car'', Car''', Car^iv, Car^v) in turns 1-8, as BAB/L/001 counts
  a column's numbers; from turn 9 it writes the turn a number was made in,
  in parentheses: "1st Car(9) − v2(6)", "3rd carr(11) − v3(8)".
- Turn 7's right-hand cell has a faint, erased line after "2nd Car''' = 0".
- Turn 9's right-hand cell: "v1" is written over another figure.

Below the table, "Carriages are negative", and eight numbered instructions,
one for each turn of two rounds, then "Now v1 has Δ² as before, v2 has Δ2
... v3 has T2 as before it had T0. The Machine stands now as at the
beginning and the same process gives Ar.Co. of T2k+1 and T2k alternately".
Quoted in the page as Remarks; a few words are uncertain.

## Checked outside the engine

The engine cannot run the sheet (no carriages, no subtraction in a column,
no fixed number of figures). Instead the 16 turns were run as the eight
instructions describe them, on three-figure wheels, and every cell the table
writes compared: 60 cells, all agree. The rounds alternate: turns 1-4 take
Δ², Δ0, T0 from v1-v3 and leave their complements in v6-v8; turns 5-8 take
those complements and leave Δ², Δ2, T2 in v1-v3; and so on. T1 = 6, T2 = 11,
T3 = 18, T4 = 27 come out as 994, 011, 982, 027.

## The pencil draft (222/657)
