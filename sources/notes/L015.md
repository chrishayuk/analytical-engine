## The cover (222/574)

Top right: "No 15", "23 Dec 1837". Bottom right "E.-1905", "BAB [L] 15".

"Notation for determining the value of x from the three equations
ax + by + cz + ∂ = 0, a′x + b′y + c′z + ∂′ = 0, a″x + b″y + c″z + ∂″ = 0 (A);
First by reducing them to two equations of the form Hx + Ky + M = 0,
H′x + K′y + M′ = 0 (B); Secondly by reducing these two to one equivalent to
H″x = M″ when x = M″/H″. Method of solution: The coeffecient of z in eqns
1, 2 of (A) are equalised; 2 & 3 ditto. Which gives the equations (B) by
subtraction. In which equations (B) the coeffecients of y are equalised.
From which by subtraction arises the equation H″x = M″. No of necessary
variables = 12; Contingent or working do = 7; Total number of
variables = 19."

Below, in faint pencil: a ruled grid of labelled rows of figures, as on the
covers of BAB/L/004 and L/005. Not read.
