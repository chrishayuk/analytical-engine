# BAB/L/005: transcription notes

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group zoom images at full
resolution (regions of about 1650 × 950 px of the 8033 × 6477 opened sheet,
and 2050 × 1650 of the cover). No person has checked the transcription.

## The cover (222/634)

In ink: "Notation For the Reduction of the Three Equations ax + by + cz + ∂ = 0,
a'x + b'y + c'z + ∂' = 0, a''x + b''y + c''z + ∂'' = 0 To Two equations of the
form Hx + Ky + M = 0, H'x + K'y + M' = 0". Top right: "16 Aug 1837 No 5".
Then "Mode of operation made use of in this Notation: (c'+c'')/c and
(c+c'')/c' are calculated", the first equation multiplied through by
(c'+c'')/c written over the second and third, "whence ((c'+c'')/c a − a' − a'')x
+ ((c'+c'')/c b − b' − b'')y + ((c'+c'')/c ∂ − ∂' − ∂'') = 0 or Hx + Ky + M = 0",
"also" the same with the second equation multiplied by (c+c'')/c', giving
H'x + K'y + M' = 0. Bottom right: "E.-1905", "BAB [L] 5".

In pencil, in a rounder hand unlike Babbage's ink: "In this method, which is
by adding the coefficients of z in the 2nd & 3rd eqns & dividing by the
coefficient of z in the 1st, thus obtaining a multiplier for the first eqn
which equalizes the coeff. of z in the 1st with the sum of those of the 2nd &
3rd & eliminates z, 15 variables are required & 26 small operations & 8 large
= 34 operations — There are 6 Multiplications & 2 divisions — see Not. 5".
The counts are taken as the page's `States`; they agree with the run.

## The opened sheet (222/636)

Heading: "Reductions of the three equations ax + by + cz + ∂ = 0 [and the
other two] to two equations of the form Hx + Ky + M = 0 and H'x + K'y + M' = 0".
"16 Aug 1837" at the top right.

Columns: "No. of Oper", "Species of op", "Results" (both under "Mill"); the
store, v1-v4 under +ax +by +cz +∂, v5-v8 under +a'x +b'y +c'z +∂', v9-v12
under +a''x +b''y +c''z +∂'', each group followed by an empty ruled column;
the operation numbers again; v13, v14, v15 (no heading); a column for "H =",
"K =" etc.; the right-hand column of formulas, headed "v1 = a v2 = b …
v12 = ∂''"; and, on rows 14, 16, 18, 30, 32, 34, a composition running on
across the rest of the sheet. Below, twice, "resulting equation equivalent
to …".

- "Com. c'" and the like are written in words, abbreviated.
- Accents above three are roman numerals (v₃ᴵⱽ, v₁₅ⱽ).
- Rows 6 and 7: the mill writes "(c'+c'')/c = A", "(c+c'')/c' = B"; the
  store "= A" in v11, "= B" in v13.
- Row 18: the species looks like "=" rather than "−"; the mill column is
  Ad − d' − d'' = M.
- Row 29: a small mark above the "−".
- Row 34's composition: after v₁₃'·v₅'' − a − a'' comes
  v₁₅'/v₇' a' − a − a'' with a slanting stroke through it (a' is written so
  that it looks like a d); left out of the page's composition. The members
  that follow use a', a, a''.
- The "resulting equation" lines put 1/c (and 1/c') before the brace of the
  x term only. As written, with the y and constant terms not divided, the
  equation is Hx + Ky + M = 0 only if the 1/c is read as applying to the
  whole left side (or dropped). Kept as `Remark`, not run.

## The back (222/638)
