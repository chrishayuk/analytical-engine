# BAB/L/011: transcription notes

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group zoom images at full
resolution (regions of 1100-1600 px, reduced for viewing; the faint pencil
with its contrast raised). No person has checked the transcription.

Five images. 222/562 is the cover of No. 11, 222/563 the opened sheet with
the two ink tables, 222/564 its back. 222/560 and 222/561 are a separate
folded sheet in pencil, the "rough draft" the catalogue says Bromley

## The cover (222/562)

Top right, ink: "No 11", "28 Aug 1837". Bottom right: "E.-1905", "BAB [L] 11".

- "Calculation of u(z+1) from the equation u(z+1) = fz . uz + f₁z", and in
  pencil after it "= vz . uz + wz".
- "And Calculation of u(z+2) from the equation u(z+2) = fz . u(z+1) +
  f₁z . uz + f₂z", and in pencil after it "= tz u(z+1) + vz . uz + wz".

## The opened sheet (222/563)

Two ink tables on the left half; the right half is ruled and empty. Columns:
"Mill" (species and result), then the store, under "Store". No numbers of
operations, no right-hand column of formulas, no counts.

**Table 1**, "Computation of u(z+1) = fz . uz + f₁z from z = 0 to z = 1 = 2
&c". v1 "f(0)", v2 "f₁(0)", v3 "u0", v4 unheaded.

- × f0.u0: v1 0, v3 0, v4 f0.u0
- + f0.u0 + f₁0: v2 0, v3 u1, v4 0
- across v1 and v2: "Calculate f(1) f₁(1)"
- × f(1).u1: v1 0, v3 0, v4 f(1).u1
- + f(1)u1 + f₁(1): v2 0, v3 u2, v4 0
- across v1 and v2: "Calculate f(2) f₁(2)"

**Table 2**, "Computation of u(z+2) = fz . u(z+1) + f₁z . uz + f₂z from
z = 0 to z = 1 = 2 &c". v1 "f(0)", v2 "f₁(0)", v3 "f₂(0)", v4 "u1",
v5 "u1", v6 "u0", v7 and v8 unheaded.

- × f(0)u1: v1 0, v4 0, v7 f(0)u1
- × f₁(0)u0: v2 0, v6 0, v8 f₁(0)u0
- + f(0)u1 + f₁(0)u0 + f₂(0): v3 0, v4 u2, v6 u2, v7 0, v8 0
- across v1 to v3: "calculate f(1) f₁(1) f₂(1)"
- × f(1)u2: v1 0, v4 0, v7 f(1)u2
- × f₁(1)u1: v2 0, v5 0, v8 f₁(1)u1
- + f(1)u2 + f₁(1)u1 + f₂(1): v3 0, v4 u3, v5 u3, v7 0, v8 0
- across v1 to v3: "calculate f(2) f₁(2) f₂(2)"

What the run needs that the engine does not have:

1. A row that gives store columns new numbers from outside the table: the
   "Calculate f(1) …" lines. Without it the second cycle of each table finds
   v1, v2 (and v3) at zero.
2. An addition of three numbers in one operation, giving its result to two
   columns: table 2, the third and sixth rows. The run stops there.

By hand, every cell of both tables is consistent with the mill entries: the
new u goes to two columns, and the two columns that hold u(z+1) and u(z)
alternate (v4 and v6, then v4 and v5), so that no complement or transfer is
needed.
