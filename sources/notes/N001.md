## Draft and fair copy compared (Sep-Dec 1834)

The fair copy (223/677) covers only Sep-Dec 1834; the draft (223/666)
covers Sep 1834 to Dec 1836. For the four months they share:

- **Columns.** The draft has 16 columns (above) and the fair copy 12.
  The fair copy has no "Signs", "Digit Counting", "Operations Counting",
  "Rack" or separate "Zero App." column; it has instead "Counting App."
  (Stor. Dec. Combin.d), "Selectors & Registering App.", "General Plans"
  split into Circular and Rack, "Miscellaneous Contrivances",
  "Notations", "Division" and "Principles". The draft's "Zero App." entry
  goes under Addition (Zero App.) in the fair copy.
- **Title.** The draft has "Steps in the history of the [?invention] of
  the Calculating Engine" across the top; the fair copy has no title on
  the sheet (the title is on its cover).
- **Addition Nos 3 and 4** (wanting tooth 12, 27) are in the November row
  in the draft, in the October row in the fair copy. Their right-hand
  figures are 7 and 7 in the draft, 7 and 6 in the fair copy. The fair
  copy adds "moveable" (pencil) and "with folding arm & screw 25" to
  No 4, brackets Nos 3-4, and adds the November note "During the passage
  of moveable tooth either driver or driven is put out of gear"; none of
  these is in the draft.
- **Addition No 1** reads "Var. End? Ungear during motion" in the draft,
  "Var. End.l Ungear during motion" in the fair copy.
- **Carriage No 1**: draft "By reversing or hoarding 9 4 3", fair copy
  "By reverg ⟨ing⟩ or hoard[?ing] 9" [right: 3] (no "4"). No 3: draft
  "with Inclined Plane", fair copy "with Do.". No 4: the draft's figure
  "25" is uncertain; fair copy "25".
- **Carriage, December**: the fair copy's note "Plan of separating Carr.e
  from fig. whs. was certainly imagined before Feb. 1835 …" is not in
  the draft.
- **Stepping**: draft "modes of stepping any number of cages but without
  power of changing that number / Mode of varying that number contrived";
  fair copy "varying" in both places and "Power of varying that No.
  contrived". The October note on carriages in the "G.t Book" is the same
  in substance.
- **General Plans, September**: the draft has "1818" only once (under
  "Circular Arrang.t"); the fair copy gives "1818" for both items, and
  for the second has "4 fig whs. in a cage" for the draft's "four fig.
  wh. in each cage".
- **General Plans, December**: the fair copy begins "Hollow [?concentric]
  wheel …"; the draft entry as read begins "Subs[?idiary] …". The
  draft's first words are not read with confidence.
- **Last column, November**: the draft's "[?Mult.] on snail [?wh]. 3 /
