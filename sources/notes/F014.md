## 4. Transcription

**Not a time chart.** F/014 rules no time rows and has no arrows, no
figures beside arrows and no stepped shafts. Its entries are labels made of
the letters "B" and "U" with a number ("B3", "U5", "U6B7"), and lines of
words. They are grouped into **nine blocks**. Each block is bracketed on the
left by a brace with a figure in the margin beside it, and on the right by a
brace with the same figure; Babbage numbered the blocks "1" … "9" in small
figures in the unheaded column k12. A block has one line of labels and,
below it, one line of words, except blocks 2 and 9, which have labels only.
F/014 therefore gets a plain tabular transcription, `block | column | entry
| note` (section 4b), not a time-chart one.

Margin figures, top to bottom (reading): **16, 12, 16, 12, 16, 12, 16, 12,
16**, the same on both sides.

Labels are closed up here ("B 3" and "B3" both occur on the sheet). "B"
without a number occurs twice (block 3, k08 and k10).

## 5. Observations that need no reading of the marks

1. **Two kinds of block alternate**, as the margin figures do. Every "16"
   block (3, 5, 7) has "Add to N.<sup>os</sup> one/two" on the left and
   "Step down multiplier" on the right; block 1 has the multiplier words
   only, block 9 none. Every "12" block (4, 6, 8) has "Step down product" on
   both sides; block 2 has no words.
2. **The additions alternate one, two, one** (blocks 3, 5, 7), the
   multiplier steps "one to two", "two to one", "one to two", "two to one"
   (blocks 1, 3, 5, 7), the left product steps "one to two", "two to one",
   "one to two" (4, 6, 8) and the right ones "three to four", "four to
   three", "three to four" (4, 6, 8).
3. **There is exactly one "Add to" between two product steps.** No block
   adds twice in a row.
4. **Labels pair across blocks.** Every "B k" is followed, in the very next
   block and the same column, by "U k" (for example k13 B5 (1) → U5 (2);
   k01 B5 (4) → U5 (5); k15 B9 (6) → U9B3 (7); k13 "U6B7" (4) → "U7B5" (5)
   → "U5B8" (6) → "U8B6" (7) → "U6B7" (8)), and every "U k" follows a
   "B k" in the block before. There are two exceptions: block 3's k07–k10
   (B1, B, B2, B), which no "U" ever follows; and block 9's "B"s, after
   which the table ends.
5. **The numbers run 1–10.** No label names 11 or 12.

### 6c. On F/013's open questions

**Which figure of the multiplier is felt?** *Reading:* F/014 contains no
word "felt", "feel", "feeler", "feeder" or "snail", no part head that F/001
uses for feeling, and no figure of a multiplier or multiplicand. The
multiplier is named only in the right group's title, "Multiplier on
N.<sup>o</sup> 1 & 2", and in the words "Step down multiplier from one to
two" / "from two to one" (blocks 1, 3, 5, 7). So F/014 does not say which
figure of the multiplier is felt, nor that any is.

**Does the table design multiply by a whole figure at once?** *Reading:*
the sheet does not say. What it does show is the order of the steps:
"Step down multiplier", then "Add to N.<sup>os</sup> one" with "Step down
multiplier" in the same block, then "Step down product" on both sides, then
"Add to N.<sup>os</sup> two" with "Step down multiplier", and so on. There
is **one** "Add to" in each 16-block and **one** product step in each
12-block; no block repeats an addition (observation 5.3).

*Inference (labelled, not adopted):* one addition per step of the product
is what a method that adds a whole multiple of the multiplicand for each
figure of the multiplier would need; a method that adds the multiplicand
once per unit of the multiplier would need several additions between
product steps for most multipliers. F/020, "Bolting plates to multiply
without table", by contrast, writes "add" five times in a row in its block
1 before a step (`F018.md` section 4). *Against:* F/014 says nothing about
what is added, from where, or how many times a unit; "Multiplication by
Table" is a title, not a description; and the schedule could be a general
pattern for any multiplier rather than one run, as F/020 seems to be
(`F018.md` section 4, "Against F/018–019"). The question remains open.

**F/013's note of 6 February 1836** ("Mult.<sup>n</sup> <u>must</u> be on
an axis with <u>one</u> wheel R as R & R1 gear with N.<sup>o</sup> 1 & 2 of
A <u>only</u> and R2 R3 are wanted for the other operation"). *Reading:*
F/014 has one column headed ′R and none headed R1, R2, R3 or A. *Inference:*
this agrees with "one wheel R", but F/013 also has only ′R (t25) among its
heads, and F/014 is undated, so it cannot show whether F/014 was made
before or after the note.

**What "B" and "U" stand for**, and **what "ca" is**: F/014 does not expand
either. Its cover title "Bolt.<sup>g</sup> plates" and its footer "Bolting
plates. Multiplication by Table." are the only words bearing on B/U. The
hypothesis in `F013.md` section 7 (B bolted, U unbolted) is not adopted
here either.

## 7. Against F/020 (reading, briefly)

F/020 ("Bolting plates to multiply without table", `F018.md` section 4) is
the same kind of sheet for F/018–019: labels and phrases, no time rows, no
arrows, grouped by braces into blocks. F/014 differs in having one block per
F/013 band (F/020 has three blocks of several lines each), margin figures
16/12 at both braces, block numbers 1–9, and the right-hand braces.
