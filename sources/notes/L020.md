under each equation; complement columns; two "Prod" columns; then the
variable cards, under "Carried over 136".

**First table**, 18 operations, reducing three equations to two. Store:
v2 B, v3 C, v4 D, v5 E; v7 'B, v8 'C, v9 'D, v10 'E; v12 ''B, v13 ''C,
v14 ''D, v15 ''E; v16 headed "C. B..", v17 "C. 'B..", a third complement

v18 and v19, and their difference into the column of the coefficient it

**Second table**, 7 operations, reducing those two to one and solving.
Store: v3 γ, v4 δ, v5 ε; v8 'γ, v9 'δ, v10 'ε; v11 "Cγ..", a second
complement column whose heading is struck out (perhaps v18), "C'γ..";

"÷ −e/∂ = z", into v12.

**Give off and retain.** A number needed again is read and kept in one

**The variable cards.** Beside each row, its cards as pairs (from, to),

## Checked, outside the engine

A script (kept outside the repository, in the session's scratchpad as
`l020/check.py`) runs the sheet's own cards pair by pair, in letters
(sympy): a pair (a, b) gives a to the mill and its complement to b, unless
a is a complement column filled in that row, when it gives it back; (a, 0)
gives a to the mill; (0, b) puts the mill's result in b. It compares every
cell the table writes after each row, every complement laid during a row,
and every quantity in the mill column.

- First table: 96 of 96 agree. γ = 'BC − B'C, δ = 'BD − B'D, ε = 'BE − B'E,
  'γ = ''B'C − 'B''C, 'δ, 'ε likewise.
- Second table: 30 of 32 agree. ∂ = 'γδ − γ'δ, e = 'γε − γ'ε.
- **The one disagreement: the last division.** The sheet writes "÷ −e/∂ =
  z" in the mill column and "−e/∂ = z" in v12. Its cards take ∂ and then e,
  (v4 0) (v5 0) (0 v12), and neither ∂/e nor e/∂ is −e/∂. The value it names
  is right: −e/∂ solves the three equations for z. As with BAB/L/001 No. 2's
  operation 12, the sign is the sheet's, not the operation's.
- Card counts: every row's number of pairs, every group total and every
  running count agrees with the cards as written (136 + 66 = 202, + 23 =
  225).

The subtractions' cards give the minuend first, (v18 0) (v19 0), as the
quantities have it ('BC − B'C, with 'BC in v18); the division's give the
divisor first.

## What the engine would need

1. **Give off and retain**: a cell for a column read and restored in the
   same operation, and one for a complement column filled and emptied in it
   (proposed in the draft as `v7 0 B'` and `v17 CB' 0`). Every × row of the
   first table but 16 and 17, and rows 1 and 2 of the second.
2. **Variable cards**: a line for the cards the sheet writes beside a row,
   checked against the run's own (`babbage cards`), with their count, and
   the group totals and running count ("Carried over 136").
3. **A repeated operation number**: the second table numbers its rows 1 to
   6 and then 1 again.
