# BAB/F/013 — "Table multiplication.", 5 January 1836

**No person has checked this transcription.** It was made by a model from
the images listed below. Read every mark against the images before relying on
it. It covers the cover, the column heads, the notes written over the table,
all four complete bands of the table and the opening rows of a fifth.

The conventions are those of [`F001.md`](F001.md) and [`F002.md`](F002.md):

- **Reading**: what is on the paper.
- **Source**: what a cited publication says, with page.
- **Inference**: this note's own guess, labelled. None is used to make the
  sheet "work".

Uncertain readings are marked `[?x]`, illegible text `[illeg.]`, struck
text `~~x~~`, interlined text `⟨x⟩`. Symbol numbers (S1 …) are those of
[`../../docs/f-concordance.md`](../../docs/f-concordance.md); the new ones
(S25–S29) are defined there, section 5.

**Date.** The date written on the sheet is **"5 Jan.<sup>y</sup> 1836"**,

written after the heads, presumably a month after the sheet (inference from
the overlap and the date).

Legibility: good. The ink is dark and the hand is careful; every word could
be read except where marked. The paper is folded vertically at x ≈ 5300 (in
the narrow column t26) and horizontally at about y ≈ 6300, and the folds
touch no mark. A water stain lies over t36–t37 in band 4, rows 9–11; it
hides nothing that was looked for. On 259 the stamp "BAB [F] 13" is at the
foot, under t43–t44.

**Identification with F/001 (inference).** No head on F/013 names the same

shared word or letter only. So the data files use F/013's own heads
(`t07 Cen Pin ″P`). Every part named in F/001's heads is absent here by
name: feeders, snails, the gathering-up tooth, the cams, the single tooth,
the registering tooth P, the starting teeth, the multiplier and complement
wheels, the multiplying pinions n…n−3, the central wheels n…n−6, X, Y, U,
C<sup>1</sup>, F.

### 4f. Machine-readable form

`../../mechanical/F013-cycle1.txt` … `F013-cycle4.txt` (bands 1–4) and
`F013-cycle5.txt` (the opening rows after band 4; not a complete band). Rows
as in section 4 (2/3 = 0; closing 1/3 = 16 or 12). Mark texts use the
vocabulary of `src/mechanical.rs`; new forms are "down arrow, double head",
"stepped shaft down, head at end", "vertical stroke, no head", "triple
horizontal strokes", and `words "B N.o 5"` for the labels. Two
consequences of the parser as it stands (not changed here): it orders
columns by a `c`/`d` number, so `t` columns sort by name only; and its
chart has 13 time units, so rows 0 and 14–16 are not drawn.

## 5. Observations that need no reading of the marks

1. **The signed figures agree.** Where a band opens with a label naming
   "B k" ("B N.<sup>o</sup> k", "B k" or "U j B k") over a short arrow with
   a figure, the figure equals the figure in the head of platform
   N.<sup>o</sup> k, and the arrow points up where that head figure is "+"
   and down where it is "−". Fourteen of fifteen cases agree: band 2 B5 ↑.4,
   B3 ↑1.1, B7 ↓.2, B9 ↓.6; band 3 B4 ↑.6, B5 ↑.4, B3 ↑1.1 (twice); band 4
   B6 ↑.2, B3 ↑1.1, B8 ↓.4, B9 ↓.6; band (5) B3 ↑1.1, B6 ↑.2. The exception
   is band (5) t23, "U 9 B 3" with "[?.6]", whose reading is uncertain.
   Arrows with figures also occur without a label (band 1 throughout; t13,
   t15 in bands 2 and (5)).
2. **Every opening arrow is closed.** In every band, each column with a short
   arrow in row 1 has an arrow of the other direction with two stacked heads
   at the end of the band (row 11 in bands 2 and 4; row 12 or 15 in bands 1
   and 3). Where both carry a figure, the figures are the same.
3. **Two kinds of band alternate.** Bands 1 and 3 (15 rows) have "Receive
   Addition upon N.<sup>os</sup> one / two" and "Step down multiplier from
   2 to 1 / one to two"; "Carry"; the t27 up-and-down pair; a second U/L
   cycle in the locks (rows 13–15); "Gears with N.<sup>o</sup> 2 / 1" in
   t25 without an arrow; and a stepped shaft in t47 (Lifters Cir). Bands 2
   and 4 (11 rows) have "Step down Product from one to two / two to one"
   and "from 3 to 4 / four to 3"; the long arrows of both axis columns t02
   and t17 (bands 1 and 3 have only t17); "Gears with … in bottom Cage" in
   t11 and "… in Top Cage" in t24; and a stepped shaft in t44 (Back Cen
   wheel).
4. **"Figures" and "Zeros" swap places.** Under the pair of figure wheels
   named in a band's words, the wheel that is moved to gets "Figures" and
   the other "Zeros": band 2, "from one to two", t03 Zeros / t04 Figures;
   band 4, "from two to one", t03 Figures / t04 Zeros; band 2, "from 3 to
   4", t20 Zeros / t21 Figs; band 4, "four to 3", t20 Figs / t21 Zeros. In
   bands 1 and 3 the same pattern runs with "Addition upon N.<sup>os</sup>
   one" (t03 Figures) and "two" (t04 Figures), and t12/t14 follow t03/t04.
   On the right the "multiplier" words do the same: band 1, "from 2 to 1",
   t18 (N.<sup>o</sup> 1) Figures / t19 Zeros; band 3, "one to two", t18
   Zeros / t19 Figures.
5. **Stepped shafts only under wheel and circular heads.** S16 occurs under
   the figure wheels, ″S2 Cir, ′S2 Cir, Carr.<sup>e</sup> c, Carr.<sup>d</sup>
   ′c, Back Cen wheel and Lifters Cir; never under a head marked "Ver". The
   long solid arrows are only in the two axes' "Cir" columns. (S16 on F/001
   is likewise only under "Cir" heads.)

### 6b. How the structure differs (reading)

- **No multiplier figures, and nothing counts down.** F/001–002 write the
  multiplier's four digits and their complements in row 2 of every cycle
  (1021, 0021, 0021, 0011, 0001, 0000), and one wheel changes by one unit a
  cycle. F/013 writes no multiplier or multiplicand figure anywhere. Its
  only figures are displacements (1.1, .6, .4, .2), signed in the platform
  heads, and labels ("B N.<sup>o</sup> 5", "U 6 B 7"). "Multiplier" occurs
  only in the right-hand title ("Multiplier upon N.<sup>os</sup> 1 & 2") and
  in the words of bands 1 and 3, where it is "stepped down" from one
  numbered figure wheel to another ("from 2 to 1", "from one to two").
- **No feeling.** There is no feeder, snail, feeler, "lowered upon" or
  "Feels upon" on F/013. Nothing on the sheet is felt, once per figure or
  otherwise. So the question whether the multiplier "is felt once per
  figure" cannot be answered from this sheet: no part on it is described as
  feeling the multiplier.
- **Fewer, longer cycles.** F/001–002 have six bands of 13 rows. F/013 has
  four bands, of 15 and 11 numbered rows alternately, plus fractional rows
  "2/3" and "1/3" at each band rule, and the opening of a fifth band.
  F/001–002's bands repeat one pattern; F/013's alternate between two (5.3).
- **Different parts.** No column head is shared (section 2). Parts only in
  F/001–002: feeders and snails, gathering-up tooth, cams, single tooth,
  registering tooth, starting teeth, multiplier and complement wheels,
  multiplying pinions n…n−3, central wheels n…n−6, X, Y, U, C<sup>1</sup>,
  F. Parts only in F/013: axes C and B (Ver/Cir), two sets of figure wheels
  N.<sup>o</sup> 1–4, ″P/″pp "Cen Pin", the S, S1, S2 and R wheels in two
  sets (″ and ′), four "Carr." columns, "Wire Axes E", three locks, twelve
  "Platforms" with signed figures, "Bolt to platforms", "Back Cen wheel",
  "Lifters".
- **The gearing words name numbered wheels and cages.** F/001–002's
  "Gears with n−k" moves on one place a cycle over n … n−6. F/013's "Gears
  with" names figure wheels by number (N.<sup>o</sup> 1, 2, 3, 4) and a
  "bottom" or "Top" cage, and returns: band 2 has N.<sup>o</sup> 1 bottom
  and N.<sup>o</sup> 4 top, band 4 has 2 bottom and 3 top.
- **Many more columns move at once.** F/001–002's most at once is 10. In
  F/013 rows 1 of bands 2–4 have 21–22 arrows, most of them the twelve
  platforms and three locks. Band 1, the first, has none of those row-1
  arrows (its row 1 has 6).
- **"Carry"** is written in both: in F's column, row 12, every cycle of
  F/001–002; in F/013 only in bands 1 and 3, row 14, in t12/t14 ("Carr.")
  and t04.
- **Marks.** F/013 has none of the marks that F/001–002 leave unexplained
  at the ends of the run ("IF", the double arrows S6/S7) and none of the Y
  marks, hatching, dotted rest lines or pencil band numbers. It has the
  stacked double head (S22) on most closing arrows, and a downward form of
  it (S25). Details are in `docs/f-concordance.md` section 5.

**Inference, labelled.** The cover of F/001 calls that method
"Multiplication before the invention of Mult.<sup>n</sup> by Table", and
F/013 is titled "Table Mul.<sup>n</sup>". The absence on F/013 of any
per-unit count of the multiplier, and of any feeling of its figures, is
consistent with a method in which the multiplier's figure does not have to
be counted down. But the sheet does not say how the multiple is formed, and
the parts that might hold a table (the twelve "Platforms" with displacements
from +1.1 to −1.1) are not described anywhere on it. This is not adopted.
