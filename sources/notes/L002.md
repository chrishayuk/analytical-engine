# BAB/L/002: transcription notes

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group zoom images at full
resolution (regions of 1100-1700 px, reduced for viewing; the faint pencil
with its contrast raised). No person has checked the transcription.

## The cover (222/622)

"Notation for the coeffecients of the powers of x in
(A0 + A1x + A2x² + …. Aix^i)/(a + bx) = λ0 + λ1x + λ2x² …. λmx^m",
"where λ0 = A0/a", "and λm = (Am − bλm−1)/a". Top right: "14 Aug 1837 No 2".
Bottom right: "E.-1905", "BAB [L] 2".

In faint pencil in the middle of the cover, read in part only:

- "1 Division gets λ0"
- "1 Multiplication", "{2} Subtraction", "{3} Bring in a number",
  "4 Division gets λ1", then something like "= 2 large & 2 small"
  "= 4 operations"
- "1 Bring in a number", "2 Multiplication", "{3} Subtraction",
  "{4} Bring in a number", "5 Division gets λm", then something like
  "= 2 large & 3 small" "= 5 Operations"

The counts are the least certain part. They agree with the table: λ1 takes
×, −, a transfer and ÷; each later λm a transfer more.

Lower on the cover, in fainter pencil: a ruled grid of four rows, each
labelled (the first perhaps "Division", the last perhaps "General"), with
0s and 1s in the columns. Not read.

## The opened sheet (222/624)

Heading: "Computation of λ0, λ1, λ2, &c from (A0 + A1x + A2x² + &c)/(a + bx)
= λ0 + λ1x + λ2x² + &c by Means of the relation λm = (Am − bλm−1)/a = Nm/a
& λ0 = A0/a".

Columns: "Number of operation", "Species of op", "Results" (both under
"Mill"), "variables" (empty), then the store: v1 headed "a", v2 "b", v3,
v4, v5, v6, v7, v8 to v14 headed "A0" to "A6". Between v13 and v14 a narrow
column numbers the rows 1 to 30 again. No right-hand column of formulas, no
composition, no counts.

- "Com a" and "Com b" are written in words (abbreviated), not with the
  looped C of BAB/L/001.
- A mark like ">" stands left of the species column between rows 1 and 2,
  5 and 6, 10 and 11, 15 and 16, 20 and 21, 25 and 26.
- Row 1 writes λ0 in both v6 and v7. Each later division writes λm in v6 and
  in the column that held Am−1 (v8, v9, …); row 30 writes λ6 in v13 only.
- Row 5: the mill's "N/a" has no legible subscript; read as N1/a.
- Row 13: the species is "−" with a stroke through it, in pencil, that makes
  it look like "+". The mill column reads A3 − bλ2.
- Row 27: "Com b" in v1 is faint, lighter than the ink of the rest.
- Row 30: v2 0 and v5 0, with no Com a in v3.
