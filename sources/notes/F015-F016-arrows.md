# F/015 and F/016 ("Table division", 8 January 1836): the arrows

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group images, November 2026.
**No person has checked these readings.** This note transcribes the arrows
and their figures on the two held-out sheets whose labels (2/3 rows), heads
and platform figures are in `f-rechecks-and-heldout.md` §4. It was written
after §4 and does not change it; the labels are repeated here only to name
the columns. Nothing in `docs/` was opened for this note, and nothing was
inferred from F/013 or from one band to another: each band was read for
itself.

**Method.** As in `bolting-plates-sources.md`: aligned 512 × 512 tiles at
full resolution (scale 1) of the IIIF zoom images
(`https://zoom.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/iiif/3/<zoom path>`), fetched one at
a time about 2.5–4 s apart and stitched locally with PIL. No request was
refused (no 403, no 429). Fetched: every tile of 216/263 (F/015 left) and
216/266 (F/016 left), and tile columns 4–10 (x 2048–5631) of 216/264 and
216/267 (the right halves, for the platforms). Regions are given as
(x, y, w, h) in full-resolution pixels of the zoom image, with the scale at
which the stitched crop was viewed; every figure was read at 0.75 or larger,
and every doubtful one at 1.2–1.6 (zooms listed per band). Crops were kept
in the session scratchpad only.

Conventions as in `F013.md`: rows are named as the left margin numbers them
("2/3", "1", "2"…, "1/3"); "11–1/3" means an arrow whose shaft starts in row
11 and whose head (or foot) lies on the band rule below the "1/3" row.
"Double head" = two heads stacked on one shaft. A figure is given exactly
as written: ".6" with a point, "6" without. `[?x]` doubtful, `~~x~~`
struck, `[hatched]` = cancelled by oblique hatching.

**Columns.** Mill columns as in §4: Axis C Ver, Cir; Figure wheels N.<sup>o</sup> 1–4;

Ver, Cir; Figure wheels N.<sup>o</sup> 1–4; ′S′S Ver; a narrow column; ′S1 Ver; Wheel
