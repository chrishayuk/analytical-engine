## The opened sheet (222/558)

Heading: "Computation of Tn = n⁴ By means of differences".

Columns: the number of the operation; an unheaded column with a cross (x)
between rows 8 and 9, 16 and 17, 24 and 25, and after 32; three columns
headed "Car"; v1 to v6 headed Δ⁴, Δ³, Δ², Δ, T and nothing; v7 to v12
headed the same; the numbers of the operations again; then a right-hand
column of words and formulas, written for rows 1 to 9 only. There is no
species of operation and no mill column.

The store starts v1 = 024, v2 = 036, v3 = 014, v4 = 001, v5 = 000: every
number has three figures, and a complement is to 1000 (976 is the
"Ar. Co." of 24).

Every row and cell is in `corpus/L010.bab`, as notes.

### How the table works (read from the cells and the right-hand column)

Each value of T takes 8 rows, as the cover says (2m with m = 4). For odd n
(rows 1-8 and 17-24) the columns v1-v5 are read and the new values are laid
in v7-v11 as complements; for even n (rows 9-16 and 25-32) v7-v11 are read
and the new values laid in v1-v5. In each block:

- row 1: Δ⁴ read; its complement into the 1st carriage, and into v7 and v8;
- even rows 2, 4, 6, 8: the next column read; the number subtracted from the
  carriage that is accumulating (1st, 3rd, 2nd, 3rd), which is written into
  the new column; and the read number's complement kept in another carriage;
- odd rows 3, 5, 7: two carriages cleared ("−0") and the kept complement
  moved to the third, and written into the next new column.

Subtracting a complement adds, so the even-n blocks use the same rule.

### Checked outside the engine

A model of exactly that rule
(scratchpad `l010/check.py`, not in the repository) agrees with 121 of the
124 numbers in the carriages and columns, and gives T1 = 1 (999 in v11),
T2 = 16, T3 = 81 (919 in v11) and T4 = 256. Three do not agree, all in the
last block, re-read at full size:

- row 28, v3: "320", where the 3rd carriage in the same row has "302"
  (108 + 194). A transposition.
- row 30, 2nd carriage and v4: both "469"; 194 + 175 is 369.

Neither affects T4: the last block's T uses Δ3 = 175, which is right.
