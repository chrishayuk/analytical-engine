## Legibility

Every entry with ink of normal strength was read; the uncertain words are
marked. Not read with certainty: the first word of the February Barrels
entry; "Mode" and "Running up" in March Miscellaneous; the first two words
of the June Selectors entry; the leading "3" in August and October
Miscellaneous; the pale first entry under December Counting; the pencil mark
under August Barrels; faint marks in the March General Plans cell. The cover
and the archive mark on the sheet were read from the overview only.
