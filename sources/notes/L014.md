## What the sheet says it is

Nothing on the sheet mentions cosines. The cover and both table headings are
about the product of two polynomials (as Rojas 2017 says, against the
catalogue's title).

## The cover (222/570)

Top right: "No 13", the 13 struck through, and "14" below; in pencil, in a

"Notations of the 7th Coeffecient of the product (A0 + A1x + A2x² + ...

q = 0,1,2,&c ...p"; below, "λ6 = A0a6 + A1a5 + A2a4 + A3a3 + A4a2 + A5a1 +

## The second table

Heading: "Calculation of the first six coeffecients of the above product";
"λ0 = A0a0  λ1 = A0a1 + A1a0 ..... λ5 = A0a5 + A1a4 + A2a3 + A3a2 + A4a1 +
A5a0". Store: v1 to v6 headed A0 to A5, v7 to v12 a0 to a5, four unheaded
columns, then v13 to v24 headed with their numbers only (a second "Store"
over v15 onward, across the fold); v23 and v24 are never used. 36 rows; no
right-hand column, no counts. Transcribed in `corpus/L014-2.bab.draft`.

The plan of the table (read off its cells):

- Each Ak is read once (rows 1, 7, 13, 19, 25, 31) and its table of
  multiples serves the products that follow: A0 for six (a0 … a5), A1 for
  five, A2 four, A3 three, A4 two, A5 one.
- Each aj is read again and again, and each time "Comp aj" is laid in a free
  column: a0 in v14, v12, v11, v14, v2 (rows 1, 7, 13, 19, 26); a1 in v15,
  v19, v12, v11; a2 in v16, v20, v22; a3 in v17, v21; a4 in v18. The last
  use of each lays none (a5 at row 6, a4 at 11, a3 at 16, a2 at 21, a1 at
  26). From row 7 on, the number read is itself a "Comp aj", and the row
  lays "Comp aj" again.
- Each product goes into a column the rows before emptied; the additions
  build λ1 (row 12, v18), λ2 (18, v7), λ3 (24, v3), λ4 (30, v9), λ5 (36, v2),
  λ0 being the product of row 1, written "λ0" in v13.

## Interpretation, not written on the sheet

- The "Comp" columns are copies kept for reuse, renewed at each reading:
  give-off-and-retain done by laying the complement elsewhere (Rojas's "0/a")
  without ever bringing the number back by a transfer, as BAB/L/001 No. 2
  and L/002 do. Whether the mill or the transfer undoes the complement when
  a "Comp aj" is read is not stated on the sheet.
- The two tables use the table of multiples in opposite ways: table 1
  (λ6 alone) multiplies each Ak once and makes seven tables; table 2 orders
  the 21 products by Ak so that six tables serve them all.
