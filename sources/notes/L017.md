## The cover (222/584)

Top right: "No 17", "1 Oct. 1838" (the day is small; it could be read 4).
BAB/L/018 is "Copied from Not. No 17 Dated 1 Oct. 1838", so 1 is taken.
Bottom right: "E.-1905", "BAB [L] 17".

"Notation For computing the elliptic radius vector by having the
eccentricity and mean anomaly". "Formula":

- ρ = A0 + A1 cos E + A2 cos 2E + &c + An cos nE + &c
- An = Bn,n·ε^n + Bn,n+2·ε^(n+2) + &c + Bn,n+2p·ε^(n+2p) + Bn,n+2p+2·ε^(n+2p+2) + &c
- A0 = a(1 + ½ε²); Bn,n = −n^(n−2)/(1·2·3…(n−1)·2^(n−1))·a;
  Bn+1,n+1 = (n+1)^(n−1)/(1·2·3…n·2^n)·a = ½((n+1)/n)^(n−1)·Bn,n
- Bn,n+2p = −(−1)^p (n+2p)(n+2p−1)…(n+p+1) × n^(n−2+2p) /
  ((n−1+2p) × 1·2·3…p × 2^(n−1+2p))·a (read in part)
- Bn,n+2p+2 = −(n²/4)·(n+2+2p)/((1+p)(1+n+p)(n+2p))·Bn,n+2p = −(Pp/4Qp)·Bn,n+2p
  (the 4 before Qp is partly a pencil stroke; with Qp as defined, P/Q is
  already the first form, so the 4 would be one too many)
- Pp = n²(n+2+2p), ΔPp = 2n², Δ² = 0
- Qp = 4(1+p)(1+n+p)(n+2p); ΔQp, Δ²Qp and Δ³Qp worked out in full, ending
  Δ²Qp = 16(n+2p) + 8(1+n+p) + 8(2+p) + 56 and Δ³Qp = 48
- "Let n = 6 p = 0": Pp = 288, ΔP = 72, Q0 = 168, ΔQ0 = 344, Δ²Q0 = 224,
  Δ³Q0 = 48 (could be read 45)

All six figures agree with the formulas at n = 6 (checked as compositions).

## Interpretation, not on the sheet

The program steps two polynomials in p, P (degree 1) and Q (degree 3), by
their differences in the mill, and uses their quotient as the ratio of
successive coefficients B, five operations a cycle: a loop written out
("loop unrolling", as Rojas puts it), with each B kept in a new column as
the result.
