four equations aw + bx + cy + ∂z + e = 0, 'aw + 'bx + … , ''aw + … ,
'''aw + … , "into" Bx + Cy + Dz + E = 0, 'Bx + 'Cy + 'Dz + 'E = 0,
''Bx + ''Cy + ''Dz + ''E = 0. "31 July 1840". In faint pencil below: "A set
of Variable Cards were made from this Notation". Bottom right "E.-1905",
"BAB [L] 19".

## The opened sheet (222/590)

Top right: "31 July 1840", the 31 written over another date (perhaps "18").

Columns: "No of Opn"; braces, each grouping three operations and numbered
1 to 4 within each group of twelve, each twelve braced together; "Species
of Opn"; "Resulting Quantity"; "Ingress" and "Egress", both empty
throughout; then the store: v1-v5 headed a b c ∂ e under "aw + bx + cy +
∂z + e = 0", v6-v10 'a 'b 'c '∂ 'e, v11-v15 ''a … ''e, v16-v20 '''a … '''e,
a blank column after each five; v21 "C.a … C.'''a", v22 "C.'a …", v23
(unheaded), v24 and v25 "Product". At the right, "No & order of Varble
Cards", one line of cards for each operation, with the number of cards in
each group of three and a running total.

Babbage writes accents before the letter ('a is a′) and d as the round ∂.

**The program.** 36 operations in three groups of twelve. Each group makes
one equation free of w from two of the four: the first and second give
B, C, D, E; the second and third 'B … 'E; the third and fourth ''B … ''E.
Each coefficient takes a pair of multiplications and a subtraction, e.g.
'a·b, a·'b, 'ab − a'b = B, the products laid in v24 and v25 and the result
written back into the column of the coefficient it replaces (B into v2,
'B into v7, ''B into v12, …).

**Give off and retain.** Where a number is needed again it is written in
its column with a small 0 above and the letter below ("°a"), and its
complement is written in v21, v22 or v23 ("Ca", "C'b", "C''a") with a small
0 below. The back of the sheet says what this means (quoted in the page).
The multiplier common to a group ('a in the first, ''a in the second,
'''a in the third) is retained throughout its group; a, 'a and ''a stop
being retained at their last use (operations 11, 23, 34 and 35).

Readings checked at full size:

- Operation 10, v24: the product is written with its accent run into the
  a, so that it reads almost "be"; read 'ae, as the mill column has it.
- Operation 13's first card is (v11 v23), as the table's C''a in v23 has
  it.
- Dashed lines cross the store above operations 13 and 25.
- Rows 25 to 36 write nothing in v1-v10, and rows 1 to 12 nothing in
  v11-v20, as the program needs.

**The variable cards.** One line per operation, e.g. operation 1
"(v6 v22)(v2 0)(v22 v6)(0 v24)", operation 2 "(v1 v21)(v7 v22)(v21 v1)
(v22 v7)(0 v25)", operation 3 "(v24 0)(v25 0)(0 v2)". Counts to the right:
"4 5 3 = 12", "= 24", "= 36", "4 4 3 = 47"; "= 59", "= 71", "= 83",
"4 4 3 = 94"; "4 4 3 = 105", "= 116", "= 127", "3 3 3 = 136". The page
carries each line as a `Cards` line.

## The back (222/591, 222/592)

"In the enumeration of Variable Cards as (v23 v7) the first variable as v23
is that which is given off the second as v7 is that which receive the
number in v23. When the first variable place is occupied by zero it
signifies that no Store Variable gives off but that the second variable
receives from the Mill." Then: "For the signification of [a small box
holding 0 over a] and the like see back of No 18" (the 18 written over
other figures; "what follows" in pencil after it) "This means give off &
retain; thus a is given off from a certain place which is reduced to zero
(denoted by the 0 above) & retained at the same place (denoted by the
position below) by means of its Complement previously put out." Pasted
slips of paper on the left.

## Run

The draft cannot run as it stands. Written out for the present engine,
each "give off and retain" becomes a read (`v6 0`) that lays the
complement (`v22 C(a')`), and each card that brings the complement back
becomes a transfer of its own (`v22 0, v6 a'`); the result C is renamed
(the notation reads C as the complement). So written (72 rows, kept outside
the repository), every cell, mill entry and named result of the 36
operations agrees, and so do the three equations: Bx + Cy + Dz + E =
'a(aw + bx + cy + ∂z + e) − a('aw + 'bx + 'cy + '∂z + 'e), and likewise for
the second and third groups: 131 statements, 131 agree.

**The cards against the run.** `babbage cards` on that version gives, for
every one of the 36 operations, the same cards as the sheet's list (136 in
all, the sheet's total), reading the engine's "C → v22" as the card that
gives off and lays the complement together. The order differs: the sheet
lists, in all 36, the numbers given off (with their complements), then the
cards that restore them, then the card that receives the result; the
engine lists the result before the restoring transfers.

## What the engine would need

1. A cell for a number given off and retained, `v6 °a'`: the column goes to
   the mill and keeps its number, through a complement laid and taken back
   in the same operation (the sheet's 0 over a; rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10,
   13, 14, 16, 17, 19, 20, 22, 25, 26, 28, 29, 31, 32), and `v22 C(a') 0`,
   a complement laid and given back within the row.
2. A `Cards` line, so that the sheet's own variable cards are checked
   against the run, card for card and in order.
3. A result named C (or a letter C) that is not read as the complement
   (operations 6, 18, 30 and the cover's equations).
4. `babbage cards` naming the source of each complement it lays: on a row
   that reads two columns and lays one complement, it does not say which.
