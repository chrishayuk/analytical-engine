## The cover (222/628)

"Notation", top right "16 Aug 1837 No 4". Three numbered entries:

1. "of the determination of x from the equation ax − b = 0", underneath
   "a great operation = 1" and "1 Division" (underlined twice); right:
   "variables = 2 / working do = 1 / total var. = 3".
2. "Reduction of the two Equations ax + by + c = 0 & a'x + b'y + c' = 0 to a
   single one of the form Hx − M = 0"; right: "variables = 6 / working do
   = 1 / Total do = 7", "great op. = 3 / small do = 2 / Total operations = 5".
3. "Reduction of the same two Equations, viz ax + by + c = 0 & a'x + b'y +
   c' = 0 to a single one of the form Ky − M = 0" (the K written over an H,
   and something small under b'y); right: "var. and opns as in 2".

Then, in the same hand: "The reduction is effected by making the
coeffecient of one of the unknowns in one of the equations equal to the
coeffecient of the same unknown in the other equation and subtracting the
results as" ax + by + c = 0, a'x + b'y + c' = 0 → (b'/b).ax + (b'/b).by +
(b'/b).c = 0, a'x + b'y + c' = 0 "whence ((b'/b)a − a')x − (c' − (b'/b)c) = 0
or Hx − M = 0". Last line: "Had H = b'a − ba' M = bc' − b'c 4 Multns must
have been made instead of 2 —— and 1 Division". Bottom right "E.-1905",
"BAB [L] 4".

Lower, in faint pencil: a ruled grid, columns headed 1 to 5 and a total
column; four labelled rows of 0s and 1s, totals read as 2, 2, 2 and 0. The
labels are not legible (perhaps Division, Multiplication, Subtraction).
Not read with certainty.
