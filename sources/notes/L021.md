## How the addressing differs from No. 19

No. 19 (31 July 1840) reduces the same four equations with each multiplier
in its own equation's first column (a in v1, 'a in v6, ''a in v11, '''a in
v16) and three shared complement columns (v21 to v23). No. 21 takes the
multipliers out of the equations: a, 'a, ''a, '''a stand in v21, v23, v25,
v27, each beside an empty column (v22, v24, v26, v28) that holds its
complement while it is given off and retained, and the equations' first
columns v1, v6, v11, v16 start empty. Two further differences:

- At the last multiplication of each group (rows 11, 23, 35) the new first
  coefficient B, 'B, ''B is moved, through the empty first column as a
  complement, into the column the multiplier a, 'a, ''a stood in. After the
  run B, 'B, ''B stand in v21, v23, v25 and C … E, 'C … 'E, ''C … ''E where
  c … e stood: the reduced equations are laid out as the four were, ready to
  be reduced again with the same pattern of columns.
- Each B is the negative of No. 19's: "a'b − 'ab = B" (here ab′ − a′b),
  where No. 19 has 'ab − a'b.

No. 19 lists 136 cards; No. 21, with the moves, 142.
