# BAB/L/001: transcription notes

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group zoom images at full
resolution (regions of 1100-2100 px, reduced for viewing). No person has
checked the transcription.

## The cover (222/593)

"Notations of Calculation". Two entries:

- "No 1. 4 Aug 1837. Elimination of x between the equations ax + by + c = 0,
  a'x + b'y + c' = 0. Equivalent to computing the value of x from the
  equation x = (bc' − b'c)/(b'a − ba'). The number of variables = 6 viz
  v1 = a, v2 = b, v3 = c; v4 = a', v5 = b', v6 = c'. working variables = 1.
  Total No of variables occupied = 7. great } operations { = 5, small = 2.
  Total No of operations = 7."
- "No 2. Aug. 5. Elimination of x and y between the equations …" with
  x = (bc' − b'c)/(b'a − ba'), y = (a'c − ac')/(b'a − ba'); variables 6,
  working 3, total 9; great 7, small 6, total 13.

The accents on the No. 2 equations of the cover are hard to make out; the
table's own heading (ax + by + c = 0 & a'x + b'y + c' = 0) is clear, and is
what the page uses.

## The opened sheet (222/594)

Columns: "Numbers of Operations"; "Nature of oper." (×, −, ÷, and a star `*`
before the two divisions of each table); "variables in Mill"; then the
"Store", headed +ax +by +c | +a'x +b'y +c' with v1 … v6 beneath, then v7
(No. 1) or v7 v8 v9 (No. 2); then the right-hand column of formulas, the
operation numbers repeated (with the stars), and the "Composition".

- In both tables the heading's denominator is read as b'a − ba'.
- No. 2, row 1, v8; row 2, v5; row 3, v9: a looped capital C before a, c, b.
  The back of the sheet defines it (below). Rojas reads the same: Ca, Cc, Cb.
- No. 2, row 7: "= x" under both v4 and v7, a dot after the second.
- No. 2, rows 8-10: no operation, nothing in the mill column; the right-hand
  column has v1'' = v1 = a, v2''' = v2 = b, v3'' = v3 = c.
- No. 2, row 12: the operation is "−", the mill column "−c − ax", the
  formula "−v3'' − v5''".
- The remark under No. 1 is in ink across both halves of the sheet; "v7 and
  v3'" are read with some doubt about the second accent.
- "Mill" and "Store" over No. 2 are faint pencil, not ink.

Pencil, not run:

- beside No. 2's right-hand column, the value of each operation in letters.
  At rows 12 and 13 it appears to read "c − a(bc' − b'c)/(b'a − ba')",
  without the minus on c; too faint to be sure.
- right of No. 2's counts: "6 Mult = 18 / 1 Div = 5 / 6 add = 1", ruled
  off, "24". The table has 5 multiplications and 2 divisions, not 6 and 1;
  6 + 1 + 6 = 13 is the table's total.
- centre, faint: "ε^(a + bx + cx² + …) = A + Bx + Cx² + …".
- foot: "085", "075 035", "6 66", and letters, unclear.

## The back (222/595)

"4 Augt 1837" and, in ink: "C, C or C before any number signifies the
Complement of that number as Ca means complement of a" (three forms of the
capital). A pencil "18" (or "28") at the top left.
