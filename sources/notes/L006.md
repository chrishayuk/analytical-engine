# BAB/L/006: transcription notes

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group zoom images at full
resolution (regions of about 1600 px, reduced for viewing; single cells
closer). No person has checked the transcription.

## The cover (222/640)

Top right "No 6". Not dated. Bottom right "E-1905", "BAB [L] 6".

"Notation For the reduction of the three Equations ax + by + cz + d = 0,
a'x + b'y + c'z + d' = 0, a''x + b''y + c''z + d'' = 0 To two Equations of
the form Hx + Ky + M = 0, H'x + K'y + M' = 0. Mode of operating used in this
Notation: c'/c and c''/c are calculated", then the first equation multiplied
by c'/c, the second written under it, "whence (c'/c a − a')x + (c'/c b − b')y
+ (c'/c d − d'') = 0 or Hx + Ky + M = 0", "also" the same with c''/c,
"whence (c''/c a − a'')x + (c''/c b − b'')y + (c''/c d − d'') = 0 or
H'x + K'y + M' = 0".

Two slips on the cover, not in the table: the first multiplied equation
has "c''/c . by" where the others have c'/c, and the first "whence" ends
"(c'/c d − d'')" where d' is meant. The ∂ (Babbage's d) is round-backed
throughout. In pencil in the margin beside the second "whence": "also".

Then: "When H/H' = K/K' = G then G = M/M' or when H/H' = K/K' = M/M'".

In a paler hand (pencil or faded ink), read with some words uncertain:
"The Notation fails — The failure is not a necessary consequence of the
method made use of in the Not — but the fault lies in the given equations.
In the method, the coeffts. of z in the 2nd & 3rd equations are divided by
that of z in the 1st — the former quot. multiplied into the 1st equation
equalizes the coeffts of z in the 2nd & the resulting eqn. This requires 18
variables — 10 small & 8 large operations = 18 Operations. There are 8
multiplications & 2 divisions —— See Not. 5".

## The opened sheet (222/642)

Columns: "No of Oper.", "Mill" over "Op." and "results", an empty column,
then the store headed by the three equations: ax +by +cz +∂ = 0 over v1-v4,
a'x +b'y +c'z +∂' = 0 over v5-v8, a''x +b''y +c''z +∂'' = 0 over v9-v12,
then v13-v18 unheaded; "Store" written three times across. Then
"Composition": the right-hand column, several sub-columns wide.

- 18 rows. Rows 5, 7, 8, 9 have no operation and nothing in the mill.
- "Comp. c" (v14), "Comp a" (v15), "Comp. b" (v16), "Comp. ∂" (v17), in
  rows 1-4, written in words.
- Rows 13-18: the mill entry ends with "=" and the store cell holds the
  letter (H, K, M, H', K', M').
- The right-hand column writes the formula first and the variable written
  last: "v7/v3 = v13", "v13·v1 = v7/v3 v1 = v7'", "v14 = v3' = v3", and in
  rows 13-18 "v7' − v5 = v7/v3 v1 − v5 = v1'' = H = c'/c a − a'".
- Rows 6 and 11: the middle member's "v11" has its second digit blotted
  (perhaps 14 corrected to 11, or the reverse). Read as v11.
- Rows 10-12 name v1, v2, v4 without accents, though each then holds its
  second number (the same a, b, d brought back).
- Row 16, mill: the a'' has its first accent touching the bowl, so it can
  look like d''. Read as a'' (the sheet's d is always ∂; the right-hand
  column has c''/c a − a'').
- Faint pencil in the empty column after the mill, about row 7: not read.
