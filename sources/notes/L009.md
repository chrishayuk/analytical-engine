# BAB/L/009: transcription notes

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group zoom images at full
resolution (regions of 1300-1600 px, reduced for viewing; the pencil with
its contrast raised). No person has checked the transcription.

Document `aa110001629`. Five images: `222/663` the cover, `222/664` the
opened sheet (8027 × 6510) with the table, `222/666` the back; `222/661` and
`222/662`, a second, pencil sheet marked "BAB [L 9 [a]]".

The table is not in the notation of `corpus/`: it computes with three-figure
numbers, not letters, and its additions are made by three "Carriages", with
the store's columns receiving the carriage's figures again and again. The
engine cannot run it (see the end), so there is no `corpus/L009.bab`; the
table is given here cell for cell.

## The cover (222/663)

Δn⁴ = 0"; "Each value of n³ takes six operations when n is even the same

## Replayed

`check.py` (kept outside the repository; its rules and the table above are
all it needs) replays the 24 rows by the rules the right-hand column states:
three-figure columns; ar Co x = 1000 − x; reading a column leaves it at zero
and subtracts its number from the carriage or carriages the row names; a
carriage's figures are laid in the store column the row names; −0 clears a
carriage. With the sheet's own −0 for the 2nd Carr at rows 7, 13 and 19, all
95 cells written (carriages and store) agree, and T1 … T4 come out 1, 8, 27,
64: as complements in v9 after rows 6 and 18 (n odd), plain in v4 after rows
12 and 24 (n even), as the cover says.
