# BAB/L/016: transcription notes

Read by a coding agent from the Science Museum Group zoom images at full
resolution (regions of 1100-2000 px, reduced for viewing; the pencil on

BAB/L/016 (document `aa110001651`) has two sheets, each a record of its
own: BAB/L/016/1, "Sheet 1" (`aa110001657`, images 222/578-580), and
BAB/L/016/2, "Sheet 2" (`aa110001662`, images 222/581-583). Both are dated

was read.

## Sheet 1, the cover (222/578)

Top right: "No 16 / Sheet 1 / June 1838", and in pencil "see also Sheet 2".
"Notation of the reduction of the three equations (1) ax + by + cz + ∂ = 0
(2) 'ax + 'by + 'cz + '∂ = 0 (3) "ax + "by + "cz + "∂ = 0 (a) to three
others of the same form as (1) Ax + By + Cz + D = 0 ... in which A = 0,
A' = 0, A'' = 0 (b), and the reduction of these last three into three
others viz (1) βy + αx + γz + δ ... (c) in which β = 0, α = 0, β' = ...".
Then "Method of Operating", begun twice: the first beginning, "Taking the
first and third of ... of the first variable viz x are eq...", is marked
"wrong" in the margin; the second, "Equation (1) of (a) is multiplied ...
variable in the second ...", is struck through with diagonal strokes; then
"The equations (2) & (3) of (a) are [multiplied by a] the first variable in
eqn (1) ...". The lower part was read only in overview.

On this sheet, and throughout No. 16, accents stand before the letter ('a,
"b) and the fourth coefficient is the round-backed ∂. The transcription
writes a', b'' and d.

## Sheet 1, the table (222/579)

"June 1838" in pencil at the top right. Columns: number of the operation;
"in the mill", what is made (a'a, b'a, ...); v1 to v12 headed a b c ∂ |
'a 'b 'c '∂ | "a "b "c "∂; a narrow column repeating the numbers of the
operations; v13 to v36. There is no column for the kind of operation, no
right-hand column of formulas, and no counts.

**First set, 33 operations.** Rows 1-21 are the products, each in its own
column (v13 … v36), rows 22-33 the twelve differences, each written back into
the first twelve columns with its capital letter:

| rows | multiplier (the table) | products | columns marked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-8 | a (v1, read at row 1) | 'a·a (twice: v13 a'a, v14 aa'), 'b·a … '∂·a, "a·a (twice: v18 a''a, v19 aa''), "b·a … "∂·a | G.R in v5 … v12 |
| 9-15 | 'a (v5, read at row 9) | b·'a, c·'a, ∂·'a, "a·'a (twice: v26, v27), "b·'a, "c·'a, "∂·'a | G.R in v2, v3, v4, v9; 0 in v10, v11, v12 |
| 16-21 | "a (v9, read at row 16) | b·"a, c·"a, ∂·"a, 'b·"a, 'c·"a, '∂·"a | 0 in v2, v3, v4, v6, v7, v8 |
| 22-33 | | (a'a) … ("∂'a), "after the manner of Bezout" | A=0 in v2, B in v1, C v3, D v4; A'=0 v6, B' v5, C' v7, D' v8; A''=0 v10, B'' v9, C'' v11, D'' v12 |

"G.R" (rows 1-12) is written in the column of the number given to the mill
when that number is kept. The back of the sheet (222/580) says what it
means: "G.R signifies give off & retain". This is the first sheet of the
series read here to have it; on the sheets of 1837 a kept number is laid
elsewhere as its complement and brought back.

Rows 22-33's mill column is in Bezout's notation, (a'a) for a'a − aa', as
the foot of the sheet says: "(a'a'') was written instead of a'a'' − a''a'
&c after the manner of Bezout" (a struck-through "Bezout has come so"
follows). Row 22's mill (a'a) gives A = 0, row 26 ("aa) A' = 0, row 30
("a'a) A'' = 0, as written in v2, v6 and v10 ("A=0").

Uncertain cells:

- Row 1, v13 a'a and v14 aa': the two orders of one product, each kept
  for one of the subtractions of row 22 (likewise rows 5 and 12).
- Row 13: a smudge, as of something rubbed out, left of v28.
- Row 14: v29 is written "−c''a'" with a smudge over the minus.
- Row 24's number: the 4 is written over another figure.
- Row 32's number has a mark before it.

**Second set: "repetition of the same process".** Headed B A C D | B' A'
C' D' | B'' A'' C'' D'' over v1 to v12, so that B is now in v1 and A in v2,
and written in part only: row 1 (B'B, v1 0, v5 G.R, v13 B'B, v14 BB'); row
21, the whole store as it stands after the products (every one of v1-v12
written 0, v13 … v36 written B'B, BB', A'B, C'B, D'B, B''B, BB'', A''B, C''B,
D''B, AB', CB', DB', B''B', B'B'', A''B', C''B', D''B', AB'', CB'', DA''
(sic: the pattern needs DB''), A'B'', C'B'', D'B''); rows 22-25 ((B'B) β=0
in v1, (A'B) α=0 in v2, (C'B) γ in v3, (D'B) δ in v4); and row 33 (β'=0,
α'=0, γ', δ', β''=0, α''=0, γ'', δ'' in v5-v12, every column of v13-v36
0). Below it:

"Here No 21 in the second set of operations shews the actual state of the
engine at that instant which will be seen to be exactly similar to No 21
set 1 the preceding and succeeding operations are also alike in the two
sets comparing the same numbers together".

## Sheet 2, the opened sheet (222/582)

"June 1838" at the top right. No table. Right half, in ink: the three
equations; "Multiply 2 & 3 by a the coeffecient of x in 1, 1 & 3 by 'a ...
2, 1 & 2 by "a ... 3. Hence arises six equations which being properly
disposed (1) f + ∂'a = 0 (2) f + ∂"a (3) f + ∂a' (4) f + ∂"a' (5) f + ∂a"
6 f + ∂'a = 0. Then (1) − (3) = {1}, (2) − (5) = {2}, (3) − (6) = {3}"; the
three resulting equations {1}-{3} written out with their coefficients
('aa − a'a)x + ('ba − b'a)y + ...; "Thus the machine has transformed the
three given equations into three others of the same form but the
coeffecient of the first variable in each is equal to nothing"; the same
for β, α, γ, δ, ending "and δ/γ = 'δ/'γ = "δ/"γ = −z". (In {1}-{3} as
written, 'aa − a'a has the subtraction the other way round from the
table's a'a − aa'; as equations "= 0" the two are the same.)

Left half, in ink, faint: "Primitive equation f(x, y, z, u, v, a) = 0" and
four more with 'a … ''''a; "put in the Machine"; "Machine puts out"
f1(0x, y, z, u, v, a) = 0 …; "put in the Machine" f1(y, 0x, z, …) …; and so
on, five times, ending "from either of which v may be determined". This is
the catalogue's "five simultaneous equations in five variables": a sketch
in functional notation, not a table. Not read cell for cell.

## Run

`corpus/L016-1.bab.draft` holds the first set, cell for cell, with the
pencil list as its right-hand column. It does not run: the engine has no
notation for G.R, and reads "G.R" as letters. Its operations would need:

- **A cell `vN G.R`: give off and retain.** The column's number goes to the
  mill, and the column keeps it; it is not left at zero and its accent count
  does not change. Rows 1-12 (v5 at 1; v6-v8 at 2-4; v9 at 5 and 12; v10-v12
  at 6-8; v2-v4 at 9-11).

A scratch copy that imitates G.R by leaving the column at zero and giving
the number straight back (an `In` row after each G.R) runs to the end: of
156 statements, 145 agree, and the 11 that do not are all accents (v2′ for
v2″, …), which the imitation's extra "In" puts out by one; with a real
"retain" they would be right. Everything else agrees: every product and
difference in the mill column, the table of a standing for rows 2-8, of 'a
for 10-15, of "a for 17-21 (three tables, as the list's v1·, v5·, v9· say),
every cell, every name (A, B, … D''), and A = A' = A'' = 0. So, with G.R,
the first set appears to agree throughout. The second set is written only
in part (rows 1, 21-25, 33) and is not run; its row 21 and row 33 agree with
the first set's under B for a, A for b, as the sheet's remark says.

`corpus/L016-2.bab` states Sheet 2's equations and quotes its text; it has
no operations, and checks nothing.
