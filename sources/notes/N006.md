## Differences between draft and fair copy

- Columns: the draft has sixteen columns, the fair copy thirteen. The draft
  has "Sign", "Digit Counting", "Operation Counting", "[?Index] Counting",
  "Registering", "Selecting", "Rack" and "Zero App." as separate columns;
  the fair copy has "Addition" (Sign · Zero App.), "Counting Ap." (Store ·
  Decl. · Combin.l), "Selectors & Registerg App." and "General Plans"
  (Circular · Rack), and adds "Notations", "Division" and "Principles".
- Draft only: "Clutch for Drum [?] 16 69 [?Cylr] Car." and "Drawing of the
  4[?] fig. Wh. ? and sign Wh. 10 74". Neither is on the fair copy.
- Fair copy only: "Iodide of Potassium for printing one copy" (August,
  Barrels); September "Algebraic Development how to print" and "First idea
  of Division in one turn"; all the November and December entries.
- Wording:
  - "Decimal & Store C. App" (draft) / "Store & Dec. Cl. App." (fair).
  - "Ditto Plan" / "Do Plan —".
  - "Opn Barr." / "Operation Barrel".
  - "Dead Stop to Fig wh. 12 Aug" / "Dead Stop to fig. wh. 12": the draft
    names the month.
  - "No 3 Grouping of "F" / "No 3 grouping on "F".
  - "Vert motion to Barrels 16 Aug. p 204" / "Vert. mot. to Barrels 16 page
    204 Vol".
  - "21 Sep p 215" / "21 p 215": the draft names the month.
  - "Grouping for Plan 18 … Do. — Plan 19 … Do — Plan 20" / "Grouping for
    plan 18 … Do ——— 19 … Do ——— 20".
  - "Rack Single Engine" / "Rack. Sing. Engine".
  - "Card backing App. p. 66 Vol IV": the same, but on the draft it sits on
    the Jan/Feb rule, on the fair copy in the Feb row.
- In the draft the large numbers (58, 60, 64, 70, 71, 75) are written in
  the same pencil, not heavier.
