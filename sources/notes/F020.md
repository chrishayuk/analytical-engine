## 3. Form of the sheet: not a time chart

**Reading.** 278 has no time rows, no row numbers, no arrows, no stepped
shafts, no numbers (no 1028, no 201, no product), no "Carry", "Zeros" or
"Figs". It has only:

- **labels**: a capital B or U and a figure, or a pair ("U5B6"), written in
  one column each (S41);
- **lines of words** written across several columns;
- **braces** in the left margin, each enclosing one line of labels and the
  line of words under it (S43);
- two heavy rules dividing it into three **blocks**.

So it is transcribed as a table of lines, not of rows:
`../../mechanical/F020-plates.txt`, one file, in the `row | column | mark |
note` form, with the first field **`bN.lM`** = block N (1–3, top to
bottom), line M (top to bottom within the block). Both numbers are this
transcription's, not Babbage's; the sheet numbers nothing. Its `rows` line
reads `rows | none: no time rows and no row numbers; 3 blocks …`.

**Notes on the whole table (reading).**

1. The multiplicand's label lines are of only two kinds besides the
   step lines: **"U5B6, P U3, pp B4, g U4, g1 B3"** (or, first time, "B5,
   P B3, ap B4, g B4") and **"U6B5, P B3, pp U4, g B4, g1 U3"**. A line
   with B5 in Turns A. is always followed by "Give off from N.<sup>o</sup>
   one Retain on N.<sup>o</sup> two"; a line with B6 by "Give off from
   N.<sup>o</sup> two Retain on N.<sup>o</sup> one". No exception.
2. **Every U follows a B with the same figure in the same column, on the
   previous label line of that column**: Turns A. B5 → U5, B6 → U6; P B3 →
   U3; g B4 → U4; pp B4 → U4; g1 B3 → U3; product B B5 → U5, 'S1 B3 → U3;
   'g1 B1 (block 1) → U1 (blocks 2, 3, after the step); multiplier R2 B3 →
   U3, C B5 → U5, "S1 B3 → U3, R2 B4 → U4, C B6 → U6, "S"S B3 → U3.
   Exceptions: **ap B4** (b1.l2, b2.l5) is never followed by a U4 in ap;
   **'g2 B2**, **'P B4**, **02 B′** and **03 B** have no U on this sheet;
   **'p'p U3** in b2.l3 and b3.l3 follows b1.l2's 'p'p B3 only at a
   distance, with several label lines between.
3. Every "add" is on the same line as a multiplicand "Give off … Retain …";
   the two alternate "from one"/"from two" from one addition to the next,
   without exception, across the block rules.
4. The product's "add" names its number wheels **"N.<sup>os</sup> one"**
   before any product step (b1.l3) and **"N.<sup>os</sup> two"** after a
   product "Step up from one to two" (b2.l6, b3.l6). The short "add"s
   between name none.
5. The product's step is "Step up from <u>one</u> to <u>two</u>" **both
   times** (b2.l4, b3.l4); the multiplier's is "one to two" in block 1
   and "two to one" in blocks 2 and 3. The sheet has no product "two to
   one".
6. **"Notice given of Stepping up"** is written only with the addition that
   comes immediately before a step (b2.l2, b3.l2), double underlined.
7. **Blocks 2 and 3 have the same product and multiplier entries, line for
   line.** They differ only on the multiplicand side: block 2 steps after
   "Give off from N.<sup>o</sup> two" (b2.l2) and its step-line labels are
   Turns A. U6, pp U4, g1 U3, then B5, P B3, ap B4, g B4; block 3 steps
   after "Give off from N.<sup>o</sup> one" (b3.l2) and has U5, P U3, g U4,
   then B6, pp B4, g1 B3. That is, block 3 is block 2 with the
   multiplicand's "one" and "two" exchanged (reading of the entries; what
   it is for is not written).
8. Counting "add"/"Add": block 1 five (l3, l5, l7, l9, l11), block 2 three
   (l2, l6, l8), block 3 two (l2, l6). Steps: the multiplier alone once
   (b1.l1), product and multiplier twice (b2.l4, b3.l4). The sheet writes
   no multiplier figure, so the counts are not tied to any number. Lines
   b1.l4–l7 and b1.l8–l11 are identical pairs.

### 6c. What bears on how "Add" and "Step up" are carried out

All quoted words are Babbage's. No meaning is given to B, U, "Give off",
"Retain", "Notice" or the part letters.

**Add.**

1. *Reading.* On F/020 every "add" stands on the same line as a
   multiplicand "Give off from N.<sup>o</sup> one Retain on N.<sup>o</sup>
   two" or its converse, and the two alternate at every addition (section
   4, note 3). F/018–019 write "Give off and retain" with each "Add to
   N.<sup>os</sup> one" (cycles 2, 3, 6), and there the multiplicand's two
   figure wheels alternate their "Zeros"/"Figs" stepped shafts (cycle 2:
   N.<sup>o</sup>1 Zeros, N.<sup>o</sup>2 Figs; cycle 3 the reverse;
   `F018.md` 6a, observation 6).
2. *Reading.* The labels before each addition decide its direction on
   F/020: B5 in Turns A. (with P B3, g B4, and first time ap B4) before
   "from N.<sup>o</sup> one"; B6 (with pp B4, g1 B3) before "from
   N.<sup>o</sup> two". On F/018, the band after cycle 1's B5 set is cycle 2
   (N.<sup>o</sup>1 "Zeros", N.<sup>o</sup>2 "Figs"); the band after cycle
   2's U5 B6 set is cycle 3 (N.<sup>o</sup>1 "Figs", N.<sup>o</sup>2
   "Zeros"). *Inference:* F/020's "Give off from N.<sup>o</sup> one Retain
   on N.<sup>o</sup> two" corresponds to F/018 cycle 2's arrangement, and
   "from N.<sup>o</sup> two … one" to cycle 3's. This rests on the order of
   the bands only.
3. *Reading.* The product's labels before the first addition are 'p'p B3,
   'g1 B1, 02 B′, 03 B (b1.l2; F/018 cycle 1 the same, with "B" in 02).
   Later additions have no product labels on F/020; on F/018–019 the
   product columns 'p'p, 'g1, 02, 03 have unlabelled ↑ in row 16 and ↓↓ in
   the next band in the addition cycles, and "Carry" in "Carriage b" in row
   13, none of which F/020 writes.
4. *Reading.* "Add to N.<sup>os</sup> one" before any product step;
   "Add to N.<sup>os</sup> two" after F/020's product "Step up from one to
   two". On F/019 the product steps "one to two" (cycle 4) then "two to
   one" (cycle 5), and cycle 6 has "Add to N.<sup>os</sup> one.".
   *Inference:* the number wheels named in "Add to N.<sup>os</sup> …" are
   those the product was last stepped to; both sheets agree with this, and
   nothing contradicts it.

**Step up.**

5. *Reading.* The same labels precede the same step on all three sheets:
   - the multiplier's "Step up from one to two" is preceded by **R2 B3, C
     B5, "S1 B3** (F/018 top rows → cycle 1; F/019 cycle 4 → cycle 5; F/020
     b1.l1);
   - the multiplier's "two to one" by **R2 B4, C B6, "S"S B3** (F/018
     cycle 3 → cycle 4; F/020 b2.l3, b3.l3);
   - the product's "one to two" by **B B5, 'g2 B2, 'S1 B3** (F/018 cycle 3
     → cycle 4; F/020 b2.l3, b3.l3), with 'p'p U3;
   - the product's "two to one" (F/019 cycle 5 only) by **B B6, 'S'S B3**
     (F/019 cycle 4).

   So in each group the figure on the axis column (C or B: 5 against 6) and
   which of the paired 'S/"S columns carries "B3" ("S1/'S1 against
   "S"S/'S'S) go with the direction "one to two" or "two to one". That is a
   reading of co-occurrence; what the plates are is not written.
6. *Reading.* After each step the same labels are released: product B U5,
   'S1 U3, multiplier R2 U4, C U6, "S"S U3 (F/020 b2.l5, b3.l5; F/019
   cycle 4 the same five). F/020 adds 'P B4 and 'g1 U1 there; F/018–019
   never label 'P (e18), and never write "U1".
7. *Reading.* "Notice given of Stepping up" is written with the addition
   immediately before each step on F/020 (b2.l2, b3.l2). F/018's addition
   before its step (cycle 3) writes only "Add to N.<sup>os</sup> one"; the
   labels of cycle 3's row 15 are those of F/020 b2.l3, the line just after
   the "Notice". *Inference:* the notice is given by the labels of that
   line (the bolting for the step is done during the last addition); the
   sheet does not say so.
8. *Reading.* Blocks 2 and 3 differ only in whether the step follows a
   give-off "from N.<sup>o</sup> two" or "from N.<sup>o</sup> one" (section
   4, note 7). *Inference:* F/020 sets out the two cases of stepping, after
   an even or an odd number of give-offs, as alternatives rather than as a
   continuing run; this would explain why the product steps "one to two"
   in both blocks and no block steps it "two to one" (if the blocks were a
   continuous run, the second product step would be expected to be "two to
   one", as on F/019). Not adopted.

### 6d. Not shared, and not to be merged

F/020 has no head, label or word that F/018–019 lack except: "Turns A."
(for F/018's "Axis A Ver"), the bare group heads "B" and "C", the label
"B′" in 02, the labels "U1" ('g1) and "B4" ('P), "Give off from …
Retain on …", "Notice given of Stepping up", and the braces. It lacks
F/018–019's figure-wheel, Ver/Cir, Carriage and "Figures upon" columns and
everything in them. F/020 is transcribed here as its own sheet; it is not
merged into F/018–019's design, and the correspondences above are by label
only.
