## The table (222/611)

All in pencil, firm and clear. Columns: "No of the Opern"; a column with a
star before each operation that makes a B (2, 6, 11, 17, 23, 29); "Nature
of the Opern"; "Mill"; then the store. Headings: v1 P0, v2 P0, v3 ΔP, v4 ΔP,
an unheaded column, v5 Q0, v6 Q0, v7 ΔQ0, v8 "0", v9 Δ²Q0, v10 "0", v11 Δ³Q,
v12 "0", a column headed "v12" with a small cross holding B6,6 (`v40` in the
page), v13, v14, v15 B6,6, a narrow column under a tear (perhaps v16, never
used), v17 to v28. Sums are written in braces ({P1}, {ΔQ1}).

29 operations: ÷ P0/Q0 and × (P0/Q0)·B6,6; then for each p, + P, + ΔQ (and
+ Δ²Q from p = 1, + Δ³Q from p = 2), + Q, ÷ P/Q, × (P/Q)·B. Each new B is laid
in two columns, one read by the next multiplication and one kept: B6,8 in
v14 and v17, B6,10 in v18 and v19, B6,12 in v20 and v21, B6,14 in v22 and
v23, B6,16 in v24 and v25, B6,18 in v26 and v27.

## The run

`corpus/L025.bab`: 93 statements, 89 agree. The P, Q and B values are
checked, as on L/018, against L/017's cover formulas with n = 6 (the Bs
without signs), defined by `Let`. All four disagreements come from row 5's
early "B6,10" in v18 and v19: two cells the division does not give, and at
row 6 the two columns found still holding a number when B6,10 is laid
there. Everything else agrees, including all six Bs, every P, Q and
difference, and B6,18 in v26.

## L/017, L/018 and L/025

The same computation, B6,8 to B6,18 in 29 operations, the mill columns row
for row the same:
- L/017 ("No 17", 1 Oct 1838, ink) does not keep Q0 at row 1 and then reads
  it again at row 4.
- L/018 (a fair copy "from Not. No 17", 9 Aug 1841) gives Q0 to v6 at row 1;
  it swaps the signs of rows 5 and 6 (× for ÷) and writes "ΔQ2 + ΔQ2" at row
  20.
- L/025 (pencil, undated) starts with P0 and Q0 each in two columns (v1, v2;
  v5, v6), so row 1 needs no copy; rows 5, 6 and 20 are right; it keeps each
  B in a fresh pair of columns (L/018 reuses v13 to v21); and its last
  division keeps neither P5 nor Q5, where L/018's does.

Which came first is not settled by the sheets; L/025 carries no date.
