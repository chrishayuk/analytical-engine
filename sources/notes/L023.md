## The cover (222/603)

"7 June 1838" twice, in ink at the top right and in pencil at the top left;
"23" in pencil inside a pencil loop with faint words, perhaps "To be drawn";
"E-1905", "BAB [L] 23" at the foot.

"Notation of a Formula for the Multn of A + Bx + Cx² + ... by a + bx + cx² +
...". "By means of combinatorial cards connected with the operation cards.
There are three groups of cards. The first makes the combinatorial numbers
(in this case the series 1, 2, 3). The second relates to the operations on
the coeffts in this case × and +. The third group relates to the use of the
combinatorial numbers. The backing apparatus Y backs the cards to the
beginning of the formulae cards. The backing apparatus Z backs them to the
beginning of the cards forming the combinatorial numbers."

## The opened sheet (222/604)

Top right "7 June 1838". Above the table, "Operation Cards" in three
bracketed groups: "Cards for making the Combinatorial number" (Diff, Table;
+; "1 Card"), "Cards of Formula" (A, a, × +; "2 Cards"), "Card by which the
Combl numbers are used" (Combl No, 1; +; "1 Card"). Each of the first and

Cards" (the first word uncertain).

The table: "Coeff." (1 to 4), "No. of Opern" (1 to 35), "Opern" and "Results
of Operation" under "Mill"; under "Store": Δ′, Tab., Combl ("This ought not
to be in Store but on Prisms"), Const., "Figure whls of Store" (empty
throughout); "Great Letter Cards" and "Small letter Cards", each Advce |
Back; "Backg Appas" Z | Y; and "Results" to the right. The first line of
the store: Δ′ 1, Tab 0, Comb 0, Const 1. Dashed lines cross the table
after each Tab row and after each subtraction that leaves Comb above 0.

Every row as written is in the transcription. In outline, coefficient k (k = 1 … 4)
is one + making Tab = Tab + 1, with Comb given the same number, then k
groups of × (a product of a great and a small letter), + (adding it to the
sum, begun at 0), − (Comb − 1). Comb's new number is written "0 1", "0 2"
(a 0, then the number); the last subtraction of each coefficient writes a
single 0 (rows 4, 11, 21), or "0 0" (row 34). Y is "back" after each
subtraction that leaves Comb above 0 (rows 8, 15, 18, 25, 28, 31); at the
end of each coefficient Z is "back" and Y "ungear back" (rows 4, 11, 21,
34). Z is "Gear Z with prism" at row 1, Y "Gear Y with Prism" at row 2 and
"gear" at rows 6 and 13; nothing at row 23. The letter cards: at the first
product of a coefficient the great cards go back (0, 1, 2) and the small
back (1, 2, 3); at each later product both advance 1. The two 1s of row 32
stand low, on the line of 33. Row 35 begins coefficient 5: 4 + 1, Tab 5,
Comb 5.

"Results": Aa, Ab, Ba, Ac, Bb, Ca, Ad, Bc, Cb, Da beside their rows, and
"Aa coeff of x⁰", "Ab + Ba coeff of x¹", "Ac + Bb + Ca coeff of x²", "Ad + Bc
+ Cb + Da Coeff. of x³".

Below the table: "NB. 252·5 hours reqd to obtain 100 terms of the Product
see p 200 Vol 3 Sketch book".

## Checked

By the engine (`babbage no L023`). The page gives the three groups of
cards. The make card gives Tab + Δ′ to Tab and the same number to Comb.
The formula cards are × of the great and small letters showing, then + into
the sum. The use card is Comb − Const. Y backs to the formula cards after
the use card unless Comb is 0, and Z backs to the make card. The letter
cards stand at Tab − Comb (great) and Comb − 1 (small). The engine runs the
cards for 35 cards and checks each row against the card in its place. It
checks the operation, the mill's result, Tab and Comb, every advance and
back of the letter cards, and every Y and Z "back". Each backing the run
makes must also be written on the sheet. The four sums, taken each time Z
backs, are checked against the four results the sheet writes, and they are
the coefficients of x⁰ to x³ of the product. All 123 statements agree.

The run also checks the NB's "252·5 hours reqd to obtain 100 terms". It runs
the cards, counting only, until Z has backed 100 times. The formula and use
cards come to 15150, which at a minute each is 252·5 hours. Counting the 100
make cards too would give 254⅙ hours, so the figure leaves those out.

What the sheet does not state, and the page therefore supplies:
- what each card gives, read from the rows;
- the 0 each sum starts from;
- what sets the letter cards' counts. The sheet writes the moves; the page
  places the cards by Tab and Comb, and the moves are checked against that.

The earlier script, `sources/checks/L023.py`, is kept as an independent
check. It reads the same rows and agrees, 35 of 35.

## Disagreements and slips

None in what the cards do. Row 23 has no "gear" for Y where rows 2, 6 and 13
have one; the sheet's own NB says the gearing is needed only if Y gives
orders in passing to its zero. Row 34 writes "0 0" where rows 4, 11 and 21
write 0.

## Against Rojas (2017)

- Rojas: backup z brings the operation cards back to the start of the
  operator loop (×, +, −), and y back to where the combinatorial index is
  increased. The sheet says the reverse, on its cover and in its table: Y
  backs to the formula cards (the inner loop, rows 8, 15, …), Z to the
  cards forming the combinatorial numbers (the outer, rows 4, 11, 21, 34).
- Rojas: the outer loop computes the first three coefficients, the inner
  loop repeating once, twice and three times. The cover says "in this case
  the series 1, 2, 3", but the table runs four coefficients (x⁰ to x³) and
  begins a fifth.
- Rojas: it remains unclear how the combinatorial index is made. The sheet
  shows it: Tab is a difference table (Tab + Δ′, Δ′ = 1), Comb receives
  Tab's number, and each pass subtracts 1 (Const) from Comb; Babbage notes
  that Comb "ought not to be in Store but on Prisms".
- Rojas: advance moves the variable cards one place right, back one left.
  The sheet's letter cards agree with that reading (small a b c d Advance →,
  large D C B A Back ←).
- Rojas reproduces the table without the variable addresses; the sheet has
  none either: its "Figure wheels of Store" column is empty.

## Interpretation (not on the sheet)

The NB's 252·5 hours is exactly 5050 × 3 minutes, with one minute for each
operation. 5050 = 1 + 2 + … + 100 is the number of products for 100
coefficients. Each product takes three operations (×, +, −). The 100
additions to Tab are not counted. The run checks this (`Time`).

## The program and the drawings (see sources/references.md)

- The Comb column of the table carries the words "This ought not to be in
  Store but on Prisms". Drawing 64 (BAB/A/064) is headed "Operation Card
  and Card Prism 19 June 1838", twelve days after this sheet. The history
  for 1838 (BAB/N/005) records "Plans for Cards 18 [drawing] 64".
- The sheet's "See Sketch Book Vol 3, p. 228, fig. 1" is the page of 10
  July 1838 (BAB/S/1/03, image 123). It shows a "Prism for Card" with a note
  of 11 July: "A is backed by Combinatorial Card, B is backed by
  Combinatorial Counting Apparatus". The facing page says "see Dr 64". The
  page is dated after the sheet, so the reference was probably added to the
  sheet later.
- The order is therefore the program (7 June), then the drawing of a card
  prism (19 June), then the sketch the program cites (10–11 July). Whether
  the program led to the prism cannot be told from dates alone. The
  sketchbook pages were seen at preview size only.
