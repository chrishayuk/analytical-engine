## Against BAB/L/001 (interpretation)

- **Not the same problem.** Rojas 2017's table lists program 26, "8.1837",
  as "two methods for the solution of two simultaneous equations". The sheet
  computes (ab + cd)/(a + c) twice; no equation is on it. Rojas's own
  worked example (his Tables 4 and 5) is (ab + cd)/(a + c), but with "0/a"
  (give off and retain) and a complement in v9, which this sheet does not
  have: his tables look like his reconstruction in the later notation, not
  a transcription of this sheet.
- **An older notation, by the look of it.** No 0 for a column read, no
  complements, no accents, no counts, no operation repeating a table of
  multiples, and arrows where L/001 writes 0. Every column receives one
  number once. L/001 (4 August 1837) already has destructive reading, Ca,
  accents and the table of b′. Nothing on the sheet dates it; that it comes
  before L/001 is a reading of the notation, not a fact on the sheet.
- **Two methods compared.** Both take five operations. The second trades
  the two multiplications of the first (ab, c∂) for one, at the price of a
  subtraction; it is the only sheet so far that sets two algorithms for the
  same value side by side.
