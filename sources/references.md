# References in the history sheets: where the cited pages and drawings are

The history sheets (BAB/N/001–008, `notes/N00*.md`) and BAB/L/023 cite
Babbage's notebooks ("Vol 2 p 84", "S.B. Vol 1 p 133", "p 406 V.IV") and his
drawing numbers (the bold figures in the notes). This file says where those
items are in the Science Museum Group catalogue, whether they are online, and
how to find the page. Checked 26 November 2026.

## The notebooks: series BAB/S

The notebooks are **BAB/S, "Sketch books and scribbling books"** (document
`aa110069648`, 1821–1870, "26 notebooks and 25 loose draft notes"). The first
series is **BAB/S/1** (`aa110000086`, 16 volumes). The series description
explains the two names: Babbage called the books "Scribbling Books" in the
notations and drawings "up to 26 Oct. 1837 after which he called them 'Sketch
Books'" (also written inside Vol III). "Vol 2", "Vol 3" and "Vol IV" in the
history sheets are the BAB/S/1 volumes with those numbers. The dates fit:

**How to reach a page.** Each image is one opening (two pages). The record's
`multimedia` list gives each image a `position` and a preview at
`https://coimages.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/<a>/<b>/large_<name>.jpg`
(1536 px wide: page numbers and most writing are legible there). The full
image is on the IIIF zoom server at the same `<a>/<b>` path. In Vol II only
the right-hand (odd) pages are numbered; in Vols III and IV both pages are
numbered at the outer top corners. Within a volume the image-to-page relation
is a fixed offset with occasional jumps where an insert or unnumbered leaf is
photographed:

- Vol II (files `large_000000NN.jpg`; position = NN + 1): image 43 = pp 80/81,
  image 45 = pp 84/85, image 89 = pp 168/169, image 91 = pp 172/173. So the
  right page is 2·NN − 5 up to about p 85 and 2·NN − 9 by p 169.
- Vol III (files `large_bab_s3_NNNN.jpg`; position = NN): image 72 = pp 128/129,
  76 = pp 136/137, 88 = a loose insert ("Notation of the Multiplication of two
  series – Sketch Book p 1…") beside p 159, 122 = pp 226/227, 123 = pp 228/229.
  Left page = 2·NN − 16 up to p 159, 2·NN − 18 after the insert.
- Vol IV (files `large_bab_s4_NNNN.jpg`; position = NN): image 36 = pp 70/71,
  206 = pp 416/417. Left page = 2·NN − 2 throughout that range.

"Viewed" below means the page was looked at in the preview image; "predicted"
means the image number comes from the offsets above and was not opened.

## Not found or not settled

- The words "Two or three sets of cards" were not read on Vol II p 173 at
  preview size.
- Vol III p 161, p 200 and Vol IV p 66, p 406 are predicted from the page
  offsets and were not opened.
- "S.B. Vol 1 p 133 / p 140 / p 118" and "Sk. B. Vol 1 p 119" (1835–36,
  N/002–N/003) are not resolved. BAB/S/1/01 ("Tool Book", 1840–42) is too
  late. The candidates are BAB/S/1/13, Vol XIII (Feb 1835 – Aug 1836, online),
  and BAB/S/1/11 (not received by the Museum). Not checked.
- "Notation for Div.n 5 & 21 [8]" and "Plan of Mult. by Table 12": the
  candidates above are not confirmed.
- Plan 28: the blog reports that the team went through the Sketchbooks and
  built a cross-reference database (Robinson). That database is not public,
  and no post found cites these pages. The SMG archive catalogue is also at
  `https://archives.sciencemuseumgroup.ac.uk/Details/archive/<priref>`
  (e.g. 110001324 for Vol III).
