% 2^2^2^n with repetition counts from number cards only, in the configuration
% of 30 May 1838: two counted groups one after the other. The first doubles
% v1 n times; its result is put on the wheels; the second squares v2 that many
% times. formal/Babbage/Classes.lean proves it for every n ≥ 1
% (`triple_halts_1838`), and proves that no deck of December 1837 with counts
% from number cards gives 2^2^2^n for every n (`no_triple_exp_1837`).
% With n = 2 the deck gives 65536.
Deck triple-1838
Title "2^2^2^2 by two loops one after the other"
Store v1 = 1, v2 = 2
Wheels 2
Expect halts
Expect results 65536
Cards
pass:
+ v1 v1 > v1
back pass counted
set v1
square:
× v2 v2 > v2
back square counted
result v2
